# 1. STRUCTURAL DECISION

**One dominant house per book.** Book One is lived almost entirely inside House Whitehart. Other houses enter as strangers, goods, law, records, and political weather.

The governing reader question remains:

> **What do you do with a voice you cannot verify?**

The book never proves the voice. It shows what belief, doubt, need, fear, and ambition build around it.

---

# 2. POV SET

- **Sela** — primary POV.
- **Brother Tomas** — secondary POV.
- **Fen** — Movement II–III; first interior access begins with an excuse.
- **Mother Alaine** — exactly three interludes: Chapters 3, 17, and 26.

**Perrin never receives POV. No fifth POV is permitted in Book One.**

# 4. THREE MOVEMENTS

## MOVEMENT I — THE TRIAL AND AFTER
**Prologue–Chapter 8**

Belief enters Whitehart and rearranges ordinary life.

- Sela is tested.
- Reverence becomes social exile.
- Alaine's hidden architecture is revealed to the reader.
- Sela sees the Ledger and understands the Unwritten.
- Col's name is spoken.
- The maintained grave opens.
- Sela's denial becomes proof of holiness.

## MOVEMENT II — THE STRANGERS AND THE RECORD
**Chapters 9–17**

The valley's memory becomes contested material.

- Perrin arrives for Blackcrest.
- Fen receives interior access.
- Merta refuses the corrected entry.
- Sela's warning becomes prohibition.
- Harl takes the neck and dies in the wet spring.
- Fen tends the bodies.
- Tomas leaves a page unclosed in time before he leaves Sela's page unclosed in judgment.
- Fen demands the use of his name.
- Alaine confronts the danger of comparable records.

## MOVEMENT III — THE INSTITUTION BREAKS
**Chapters 18–27**

Law, hunger, record, and public need exceed Whitehart's control.

- Orla applies the boundary.
- Perrin's methods continue after he leaves.
- The gate violence produces two death counts and irreconcilable memory.
- Wilda reveals that she let Col go.
- Duskport carts feed the valley.
- Roan and Ottar carry Stormrider pressure.
- A false quotation becomes doctrine.
- Sela receives no verdict.
- Alaine does not give the order that would stop her.
- Sela walks east with Harl's knife.

# 7. LOCKED CAUSAL CHAINS

## Harl

- Sela warns him about wet ground.
- She refuses to claim the dead spoke.
- Money, public standing, Corr's bookings, and Tam's promised horse matter.
- Harl takes the neck, the safer route.
- The wet spring takes the shelf.
- The voice is not made an executioner.

## The gate

- The crowd is unarmed.
- Hunger, administrative failure, fear, and institutional choices escalate.
- Official count: nine.
- Valley count: twelve.
- Three remain unreconciled.
- Wick remains saint / butcher depending on the account.
- Ordin Vass saves two people and fits no clean version.
- Three are compensated and six are not.

# 8. END IMAGE

Sela walks east beyond the mapped road while Garron's death settles into political fact behind her.

The ending must not:

- verify the voice;
- reveal the forgotten eleventh;
- promise that east contains an answer;
- resolve Col;
- let Sela learn Fen's betrayal.

The final lines are locked:

> The map ended.  
> She kept walking.
