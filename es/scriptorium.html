The Scriptorium

What readers leave for the archive: a story from the Banner-lands, a theory about the Bell, a piece of art, a question for the author. The archivist reads everything — the finest pieces are honoured, and the very finest are taken into the material around the book.

Content note. Aurefold is a mature series; submissions may explore adult themes. Everything is reviewed before it appears — gratuitous or hateful material is declined.
