The Banner Hall

Los personajes que los lectores han jurado a las Banner-lands. Viven alrededor de la historia — en los márgenes, los pueblos de las colinas, los caminos de peaje — y el archivista lee cada uno antes de que ocupe su lugar en la sala.

The Banner Hall is where reader-created characters are sworn into the world around Aurefold — each raised under one of the ten houses, each read by the archivist before it takes its place. Soldiers, scribes, and smugglers of secrets gather here; the finest are honoured, and the very finest are taken into the material around the book.
