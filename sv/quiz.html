Which House would you follow?

Twelve moral dilemmas. Ten philosophies of how people should live together. There is no correct House here: every answer protects something worth protecting, and every answer asks somebody to pay a price.

Blackthorn trusts intellect, Ashbourne courage, Whitehart faith, Stormrider unity, Ravenshade information, Ironvale innovation, Blackcrest the victor's history, Stonebear honor, Tidebreaker knowledge, and Phoenix the future. The test finds the philosophy you lean toward — and shows the cost built into it.
