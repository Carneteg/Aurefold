The Ledger Question is Aurefold's recurring argument: one difficult moral choice, defensible answers, and no official verdict. Add your voice, then come back to see whether the community divided where you expected.

THE LEDGER QUESTION

These questions are built from the same tension that governs Aurefold — how people should live together when every principle protects something real and creates a cost somewhere else. They are community dilemmas, not hidden canon tests.

One question each week. A new Ledger Question opens every Wednesday at 19:00 Europe/Stockholm. The previous record closes as the next one opens.

The Ledger never declares which answer is morally correct. The interesting part is the argument readers make after choosing.

The wider Moot also lets readers shape bounded material around the books: which supplemental archive fragment opens next, what art gets explored, or which non-canon extra receives attention. The central plot, canon, character fates and protected mysteries remain the author's.

Want the next Ledger Question when it opens? Join Aurefold free — no canon paywall, just the next argument, art and production notes.
