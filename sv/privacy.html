Integritet & cookies

Rakt på sak: vad Aurefold samlar in, varför, och hur du behåller kontrollen.

Aurefold is a small promotional and reader-community site for the novel The Bell of Silence. We keep data collection to a minimum and set no non-essential cookies until you agree. You can change your mind at any time.

Cookies & ditt samtycke

When you first visit, a banner asks whether to allow cookies. Nothing that sets a non-essential cookie runs until you choose Accept:

  • Statistik (Google Analytics 4). Anonym, aggregerad användning — vilka sidor som besöks, ungefär varifrån besökarna kommer, och händelser som en bok- nedladdning. Den körs i Googles Consent Mode med lagring nekad som standard, så den sätter inga statistik-cookies om du inte accepterar.
  • Läslistans formulär (MailerLite). Om du accepterar laddas e-postformuläret på Stöd-sidan från MailerLite, som kan sätta egna cookies. Om du nekar laddas formuläret helt enkelt inte.

Decline and Google Analytics stays cookieless and the email form isn't loaded — the rest of the site works exactly the same.

Community participation stored in your browser

Interactive Aurefold features use a small amount of browser local storage so the site can remember community choices on that browser without asking for a name or email. This is separate from Google Analytics cookies.

  • A random browser identifier is used to enforce one anonymous voice per Moot question and to avoid counting the same House Test result repeatedly.
  • Your local browser remembers which Ledger Questions you answered so the Moot can show your own participation record when you return.
  • This local record is not a score, profile or public leaderboard, and it does not follow you to another browser or device.

Anonymous community milestones

When you actively take part in the community, Aurefold may store a limited anonymous milestone in Supabase: completing the House Test, casting a Ledger Question vote, having a signed-in Banner profile, or clicking through to Patreon. The record contains the random browser identifier, the type of milestone, a short context label and the Aurefold page where it happened.

It does not store your House Test answer sequence, the text of your Ledger argument, your Patreon account, whether you actually joined Patreon, or whether a Patreon membership is free or paid. Patreon clicks are treated only as an indication that a reader chose to open Patreon.

Uppgifter du själv väljer att lämna

  • E-postadress — bara om du går med i läslistan. Den används för att skicka Aurefold-uppdateringar, och du kan avprenumerera från vilket mejl som helst när som helst.
  • Reader account / Banner profile — if you sign in, your account and the profile information you choose to save are stored by Supabase.
  • Omdömen — om du lämnar ett betyg eller omdöme om Bok Ett lagras det frivilliga namnet och texten du anger och visas publikt när de godkänts.
  • The Moot votes — votes are stored anonymously to tally results. Anonymous votes are not tied to a named reader account.

Vem som behandlar dessa uppgifter

  • Google (Analytics) — mätning av användning.
  • MailerLite — e-post för läslistan.
  • Supabase — reader accounts, Moot votes, community milestones and Book One reviews.
  • Patreon — only after you choose to open Patreon; Patreon handles its own account and membership data.
  • GitHub Pages — är värd för sajten; serverloggar kan registrera vanliga förfrågningsdata (som IP-adresser) som en del av normal drift.

Dina rättigheter

You can withdraw cookie consent at any time (the button above), unsubscribe from emails, and request that we correct or delete personal data you've given us (such as your email, reader account/profile or a review). To make a request, email us at info@aurefold.com.
