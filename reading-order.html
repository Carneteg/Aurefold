Reading order

A short note on where to begin, and in what order the archive is meant to be read.

Begin here: Book One — The Bell of Silence

The series begins with The Bell of Silence — some 106,700 words, forty-seven chapters and an interlude, complete and out now as a free ebook. A goatherd in a mountain valley hears a voice she will not explain, and a house that exists to test such testimony comes to her door. Everything in Aurefold — the ten houses, the compact they keep, the record they dispute — starts in that valley. Nothing is required before it, and nothing in this note spoils it.

Then: Book Two — The Road Still Open

The Road Still Open is forthcoming. It continues directly from the end of Book One and is best read after it — the second volume assumes you know what the record said, and what it left out. When it arrives it will be announced on the book page and in the journal.

At any point: the shorter works

The novellas and shorter texts in the archive are stand-alone. They are not installments and carry no required order; each is a single record, complete in itself, set in the same world. Read them whenever curiosity takes you — before Book One, between books, or after. Readers who want to test their footing first can begin with the opening chapters of Book One, free to read online.
