The Moot

An old word for an assembly where every voice is counted. One voice per reader.

The Moot shapes presentation — which supplemental material, visual priority, or public topic the archive opens next. It never decides canon, plot, character fates, or the book’s unresolved mysteries. Those remain the author’s.
