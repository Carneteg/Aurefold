The Moot

An old word for an assembly where every voice is counted. One voice per reader — and while the Moot is young, early votes show as a standing; the exact counts open once enough readers have weighed in.

The Moot shapes the world around the book — which supplemental material, art, or public topic the archive opens next, and bounded extras like which side-story gets written or the name of a minor figure in the supplementary lore. The central plot, canon, character fates, and the book’s unresolved mysteries stay the author’s.
