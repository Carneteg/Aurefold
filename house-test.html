THE BANNER · HOUSE TEST

Which Great House are you?

Twelve choices. None clean. None officially correct. The test asks what you protect when every defensible choice costs something.

This is a reader-community test, not an in-world judgment and not canon. Aurefold never declares one House morally right.

The Houses do not divide people into heroes and villains. Each offers a way humanity might live together — and each creates both shelter and victims.

No name or email is required. One anonymous result per browser may be counted in the community distribution.
