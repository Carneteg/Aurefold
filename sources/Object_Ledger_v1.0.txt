1. Governing object rules
An object may carry evidence, labor, custody, debt, ritual, memory, or legal effect without proving objective history.
Custody changes must be recorded when they alter character power or cross-book continuity.
No object may independently prove magic, Col’s fate, or the erased Eleventh House.
