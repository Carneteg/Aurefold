Authority status: GOVERNING BOOK-SPECIFIC CHRONOLOGY. Relative sequence is controlling where exact dates are not yet named. Book One begins in 396 A.U., four years before the Jubilee.
1. Chronology principles
No Gregorian month or Christian feast naming is used.
Relative durations stated in prose control over inferred calendar placement.
The prologue occurs early enough for Perrin’s twenty-nine-day journey and later Whitehart events within 396 A.U.; an exact date is not publication-locked.
Book One ends with Sela east of Whitehart; she reaches Vey Crossing on the tenth travel day under cross-book continuity, but that journey is outside Book One.
