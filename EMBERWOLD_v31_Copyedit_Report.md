## Copy-edit scope

This was a copy-edit and continuity pass, not a broad prose rewrite. The pass addressed:

- spelling and house style (British English),
- punctuation and dialogue quotation consistency,
- duplicated or contradictory phrases,
- local grammar and agreement,
- internal chronology,
- chapter order and heading consistency,
- named-character ages and identifiers,
- facts that contradicted the current lore authority.

The existing v30 voice, rhythm and scene architecture were preserved wherever no correction was required.

## Principal copy-edit corrections

1. Standardised curly quotation marks and apostrophes throughout.
2. Standardised British forms, including `towards`, `afterwards`, `grey`, `centre`, `licence`, `defence` and `authorised`.
3. Removed local duplications in Chapters 2, 8 and 22.
4. Corrected a grammatical agreement error: `These are poor travelling food` → `This is poor travelling food`.
5. Corrected the Ottar introduction so Sela does not both “never learn” his name and later recall Nella using it.
6. Restored a missing scene separator before Chapter 26.
7. Corrected the Chronicle dating of Harl's death from the four-hundredth year to **396 A.U.**
8. Preserved all deliberate fragments, repetitions and failed jokes that belong to POV voice rather than copy error.

## Principal lore corrections

3. Standardised the date line to **396 A.U.**
4. Corrected the Unwritten Ledger page so it carries a date and unfinished entry but **no name**.

10. Preserved Ottar's age (**38**) and all three unexplained signs: separate cup/service order, difficulty using `I`, and the mended horsehair braid.
11. Preserved the knowledge boundary in Chapter 25: Sela leaves without learning that Fen sold information about her fear.
