## 1. External beta reading

Recruit readers who can separately assess:

- literary fantasy / prose;
- plot clarity and emotional attachment;
- fantasy world comprehension;
- whether the prologue helps or delays engagement;
- whether the Ten-house frame is clear without memorization;
- Fen, Alaine, Harl, Merta, and Sela as reader-bond characters.

Do not ask beta readers to rewrite. Ask where attention drops, what they misunderstand, and which choices feel earned.

## 2. Feedback triage

Classify every beta note as:

- prose / clarity;
- pacing;
- character motivation;
- lore contradiction;
- intended ambiguity misunderstood;
- personal preference.

A beta note does not alter canon automatically.

## 3. Human proof / typesetting proof

After beta changes:

- spelling and punctuation proof;
- chapter-heading consistency;
- typography and scene-break consistency;
- widows, orphans, page breaks, and italics;
- final word count and front matter.
