# 2. FRONT-COVER CANON CHECK

The front-cover concept is broadly compatible with Book One:

- mountain monastery / Bell of Silence;
- Whitehart stag device;
- solitary eastward traveler;
- low-key, dark, non-spectacle fantasy tone.

Potential design cautions:

- the traveler should remain identifiable as Sela without becoming a glamorous warrior figure;
- the bell and monastery should not imply a conventional church hierarchy stronger than Whitehart's actual institution;
- no visual eleven-count motif should be added casually;
- avoid overt magical light, ghosts, or a visibly supernatural voice.

---

# 3. BACK-COVER COPY ISSUE

The current back-cover concept includes language equivalent to:

> “the voice that guided Sela … only points east.”

This is too definitive for v27.0 canon. The book never proves that a voice guides her or that an external voice points east.

A future back-cover version should preserve ambiguity. Safe framing:

- Sela followed a direction in winter and people survived;
- during her testing she turns east;
- Whitehart, the valley, and later histories decide what that means;
- Sela herself never receives certainty.

The back cover also mentions the erased eleventh directly. This is acceptable as series marketing only if the author wants the long mystery disclosed in jacket copy; it is not required for Book One's immediate premise.
