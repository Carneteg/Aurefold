# EMBERWOLD — CHARACTER ATLAS UPDATE BRIEF

## Version 27.0

Use this brief to rebuild the English character atlas after Book One canon closure.

---

# PURPOSE

Create a character atlas that distinguishes:

1. people the reader meets in Book One;
2. figures who exist in wider series canon but have not yet entered dramatized prose;
3. held figures who must not be presented as settled canon.

The atlas is a reference and visual identity document, not a lore-expansion vehicle.

---

# REQUIRED BOOK ONE PAGES

## Sela
- 19; former goatherd; Called under testing.
- Rough practical clothing, not ceremonial splendor.
- Harl's knife becomes a late-book object.
- Visual tell: faces openings when unobserved.
- Do not illustrate a visible supernatural voice.

## Tomas
- 45; sixteen years a Tester, nine as the Tester.
- Char, paper, lamp, burned notes.
- Hands and observational posture matter more than priestly grandeur.

## Alaine
- ~60; fifty years of authority; never heard.
- Back against stone / dead behind her.
- Stillness should be readable as authority and defense.
- No ghostly aura.

## Fen
- 24; Unwritten for five years; niche-tender.
- Wax, bone knife, candle-box, work-worn hands.
- Must look socially present and institutionally erased, not ethereal.

## Merta
- 52; Col's mother.
- Coat carried at first snow; boundary stone.
- Grief without saintly beautification.

## Wilda
- Senior legal authority.
- Controlled, precise, practical.
- No villain coding.

## Perrin
- Blackcrest Jubilee Herald.
- Good coat, road wear, writing materials.
- Charming, useful, attentive; not sinister caricature.

## Harl
- Valley worker and guide.
- Large practical hands, maul, straps, fence work.
- Warmth and pride must coexist.

## Ivet
- 16; sincere novice believer.
- Young, ordinary, emotionally open.
- Do not make her knowingly manipulative.
