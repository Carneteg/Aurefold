Privacy & Cookies

Plain terms: what Aurefold collects, why, and how you stay in control.

Aurefold is a small promotional site for the novel The Bell of Silence. We keep data collection to a minimum and set no non-essential cookies until you agree. You can change your mind at any time.

Cookies & your consent

When you first visit, a banner asks whether to allow cookies. Nothing that sets a cookie runs until you choose Accept:

  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4). Anonymous, aggregated usage — which pages are visited, roughly where visitors come from, and events like a book download. It runs in Google's Consent Mode with storage denied by default, so it sets no analytics cookies unless you accept.
  • Reading-list form (MailerLite). If you accept, the email signup form on the Support page loads from MailerLite, which may set its own cookies. If you decline, the form simply isn't loaded.

Decline and the analytics stays cookieless and the email form isn't loaded — the rest of the site works exactly the same.

Information you choose to give

  • Email address — only if you join the reading list. It's used to send Aurefold updates, and you can unsubscribe from any email at any time.
  • Reviews — if you leave a rating or review of Book One, the optional name and text you enter are stored and shown publicly once approved.
  • The Moot votes — votes are stored anonymously to tally results. They are not tied to your identity.

Who processes this data

  • Google (Analytics) — usage measurement.
  • MailerLite — reading-list email.
  • Supabase — stores Moot votes and Book One reviews.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the site; server logs may record standard request data (such as IP addresses) as part of normal operation.

Your rights

You can withdraw cookie consent at any time (the button above), unsubscribe from emails, and request that we correct or delete personal data you've given us (your email or a review). To make a request, reach us through the Aurefold Patreon.

This notice may be updated as the project grows. Last updated: 11 August 2026.
