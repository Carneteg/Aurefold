The Ten Great Houses

Each house is a philosophy of leadership — a promise, and its cost. The question is never which is good; it is which you would follow. Every house here carries the one question its virtue forces on everyone else. Named leaders are held back from these pages until the book is published.

Which of these would you follow?

Not sure where you'd swear your oath? Six questions will tell you.
