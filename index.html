EMBERWOLD

The map ends where the truth begins.

▾

Four hundred years have passed since the War of the Unification ended and the year was set to nought. Ten Great Houses have ruled the Banner-lands ever since — the present year is 396 A.U. — and each of them is less a family than a philosophy of leadership: a wager about what people are, and what they can be asked to become.

The founding pact, the Ash Oath, bears ten seals — some say eleven.

The question these books ask is never which house is good. It is: which house would you follow?
