# 1. Positioning

## Page name
**AUREFOLD**

Do not add “official”, “author”, “support page”, or “fantasy project” to the page name. The brand is stronger on its own.

## Discovery / short description
**Epic fantasy, worldbuilding, and the road from finished manuscript to print.**

Alternative if a more literary tone is needed:
**Ten houses. Ten philosophies of rule. One unfinished history.**

## Brand line
**The map ends where the truth begins.**

## Core Patreon promise
Patreon is the **inside-the-workshop layer** of Aurefold.

It is not:
- a donation page;
- a place where readers buy canon;
- a paywall around the entire novel;
- a substitute for the future crowdfunding campaign.

It is where readers get closer access to:
- the making of the books;
- the archive behind the world;
- annotated craft material;
- character and visual development;
- House/institution deep-dives;
- publication progress;
- bounded non-canonical community participation.

# 3. About section — READY TO PASTE

## Recommended version

**AUREFOLD is an epic fantasy series about ten Great Houses, ten philosophies of rule, and the people who have to live beneath them.**

Book One, *The Bell of Silence*, is complete. Here on Patreon, I open the archive behind the work: annotated scenes, House and institution deep-dives, worldbuilding documents, character art, development notes, reader Q&As, and the road from finished manuscript to a professionally produced print edition.

Join free as a **Traveller** for publication milestones, selected excerpts, world introductions, and first notice when the print campaign opens.

Paid members get a closer look at how Aurefold is built — including craft notes, deeper archive material, early visual work, production updates, and member-only discussions.

Membership does **not** buy authority over the story. Canon, character fates, and unresolved mysteries remain the author's responsibility. Community votes may shape which supplementary material opens next, but never the truth of the books.

**If you want to watch a finished novel become a published world — and step inside the archive while it happens — you are in the right place.**

# 11. Recommended publishing cadence

The page will look more professional if the promise is predictable and sustainable.

Recommended baseline:
- **1 public/free project post per month**
- **2 paid posts per month**
  - one craft / behind-the-scenes item;
  - one world / character / archive item;
- **1 quarterly written Q&A** for Chronicler+;
- additional milestone posts only when something real changes.

Do not promise weekly material unless you are willing to maintain it for at least a year.

The professional signal is consistency, not volume.

# 17. Professionalism issues to avoid

1. **Outdated public facts.** The Aurefold public site currently contains older manuscript word-count language in places. Patreon should either use the current governing figure or, preferably in evergreen About copy, omit a precise word count.
2. **Too many explanations of what membership is not.** Keep the canon boundary concise on Patreon; the long governance explanation belongs on Aurefold's support/FAQ pages.
3. **Benefits that overlap without progression.** Every paid tier should add a clearly different depth of access.
4. **Unsustainable high-tier promises.** No custom chapters, monthly calls, bespoke lore, or personal commissions unless deliberately resourced.
5. **Empty paid archive.** Do not promote three paid levels unless each level can immediately unlock representative material.
6. **Fundraising-first language.** Lead with the world, book, access, and experience — not expenses.
7. **Crowdfunding confusion.** Patreon = ongoing membership / behind the work. Future crowdfunding = physical-production campaign and reward fulfilment.
