## 2. PATREON ABOUT — REPLACE CURRENT TEXT WITH THIS

**Aurefold is a political and literary epic fantasy series built around one question: how should humanity live together?**

For four centuries, ten Great Houses have defended different answers. Faith. Courage. Intellect. Unity. Information. Innovation. Honor. Knowledge. History. The future.

None of them are entirely right. None of them are harmless.

Book One, **The Bell of Silence**, follows Sela of the Light Heights, a working woman who wants to return to the ordinary life she understands. Instead, uncertainty around her becomes public meaning. Pilgrims arrive. Records multiply. Institutions compete to protect, interpret, preserve and use what they believe is happening.

Aurefold is fantasy built through people before spectacle: labor, law, faith, roads, food, testimony, violence, memory and consequence. Magic is never objectively confirmed. History never belongs to a single witness. The world is designed so that every great political idea can be defended by a decent person — and can still create victims.

This Patreon is the **author archive and publication room** for Aurefold.

Members get access to selected material from the making of the series: character art, worldbuilding, production notes, manuscript and publication updates, early visual work, behind-the-scenes decisions, and deeper archive material as the project moves from completed Book One manuscript toward professional publication and print.

Membership can influence **how supplemental material is presented** — never canon, plot outcomes, character names, or editorial authority.

If you want to follow the project casually, join free. If you want to go deeper into the archive, choose the membership level that fits you.

**The map ends where the truth begins.**

Explore Aurefold at **aurefold.com**.
