# Aurefold — Community, Fandom & Patreon Growth Strategy v1.0

**Status:** GOVERNING GROWTH / COMMUNITY STRATEGY — NOT CANON  
**Owner decision:** Approved for implementation, 19 August 2026.  
**Canon rule:** Nothing in this document creates fictional canon. Canon authority remains in `canon/SOURCE_REGISTRY.md` and the active Constitution/Ledger stack.

## North star

Do not market Aurefold as only a book. Build a fandom in which readers develop **identity, participation, rituals, relationships and memory** around the world before asking them to pay.

The community funnel is:

**Discovery → Identity → Participation → Community → Habit → Support**

Or, in concrete Aurefold terms:

**“I saw a video” → “I took the House Test” → “I am Blackthorn” → “I argued with a Whitehart” → “I started reading” → “I joined free” → “I wait for the next Aurefold ritual” → “I want to support this.”**

Patreon is the monetisation layer of an existing sense of belonging, not the first step.

## Strategic asset: the Ten Great Houses

The Houses are Aurefold’s strongest community identity system because each House represents a defensible way of living together rather than a simple personality type or good/evil faction.

- Blackthorn — Intellect
- Ashbourne — Courage
- Whitehart — Faith
- Stormrider — Unity
- Ravenshade — Information
- Ironvale — Innovation
- Blackcrest — The Victor’s History
- Stonebear — Honor
- Tidebreaker — Knowledge
- Phoenix — The Future

**Community objective:** readers should eventually say which House they belong to. House identity must remain playful/community-facing and must never imply that one House is morally correct.

## Priority 1 — “Which House are you?”

Build a shareable House Test on aurefold.com.

### Product rules

- 10–15 morally difficult dilemmas, not superficial personality questions.
- Every answer must have a defensible moral rationale and a real cost.
- No result is “best.”
- Avoid obvious question-to-House mapping; score multiple Houses per answer.
- Show a primary House plus a close secondary result when meaningful.
- Result page should include House name, philosophy, short interpretation, link to the House page, share action, and direct routes to The Banner and free Patreon membership.
- Store anonymised result distribution in Supabase so the site can later show fandom House distribution.
- House quiz results are **community metadata, not canon**.

### Conversion goal

The test is the bridge from anonymous discovery to identity. A result should naturally prompt: **“Take your place under the Banner.”**

## Priority 2 — The Ledger Question

Create a weekly recurring ritual: one Aurefold moral dilemma with two or more defensible answers and no official correct answer.

Examples should revolve around the series’ governing question — **How should humanity live together?** — without resolving protected canon mysteries or previewing unreleased facts as truth.

Distribution:

- TikTok
- YouTube Shorts
- Instagram Reels
- aurefold.com / The Moot
- Patreon

The comments and voting results become input for the next week’s content.

### Editorial rule

Questions may be inspired by canonical tensions, but community hypotheticals must be clearly framed as hypotheticals when they are not events from the books.

## Priority 3 — “Nine / Twelve” as a recurring format

Turn contradictory evidence into a repeatable Aurefold social format:

- two witnesses;
- two maps;
- two records;
- two House interpretations;
- two explanations that cannot both be fully reconciled.

End with a participation prompt such as **“Who do you believe?”** or **“What would you record?”**

This should express Aurefold’s epistemic identity without turning mysteries into solvable puzzles with canonical answers.

## Priority 4 — Character fandom before lore mastery

Build recurring formats around characters before expecting audiences to learn institutions or chronology.

Examples:

- Sela’s grounded institutional skepticism and practical intelligence.
- Tomas’s exactness, pedantry and accidental humor.
- Alaine’s intelligent judgment and satirical condescension.
- Future character-led formats as their public-safe material becomes available.

Objective: readers should be able to say **“Tomas is my favorite”** before they can explain the Tenfold Compact.

## Priority 5 — Patreon free membership first

Use Patreon as a community funnel, not only as a payment wall.

### Free member experience

- The Ledger Question / Moot prompts.
- Artwork and production notes.
- Development updates.
- Selected behind-the-scenes material.
- Invitations into community rituals.

### Paid member principle

Paid support buys **closeness, process, access and recognition** — not control over canon and not the core story being hidden away.

Recommended initial paid structure:

- ~€4 entry supporter tier.
- ~€8 deeper behind-the-scenes / annotated material tier.
- ~€15 inner-circle access / live creator Q&A / early media tier.

Exact names and benefits can evolve. Do not create excessive tiers.

Potential paid benefits:

- annotated chapters;
- creator commentary;
- worldbuilding process;
- alternate/deleted passages clearly marked NON-CANON;
- early artwork/video access;
- monthly creator Q&A;
- deeper theory/community discussions.

**Hard rule:** money never buys a Canon Lock, a character fate, the solution to a protected mystery, or authority over the published story.

## Priority 6 — Community architecture

Use the existing Aurefold community infrastructure before creating more empty channels.

Current site assets already include:

- The Banner — reader community hub;
- The Moot — reader voting;
- Banner Hall — fan characters;
- Scriptorium — stories, theories, art and questions;
- Supabase member/profile/community data;
- Patreon support link.

Strengthen these first. Do not create a sprawling Discord structure before activity justifies it.

## Priority 7 — Founding Readers

Create a real-world community recognition program for the first cohort of paying supporters, e.g. first 100 or 250.

- Permanent digital “Founding Reader” recognition on the website/Patreon.
- This is a fandom/community distinction only.
- It confers no in-world noble title, fictional authority or canon status.

## Priority 8 — Make fandom activity visible

As real participation grows, surface honest aggregate community signals:

- House distribution;
- current Ledger Question standings;
- most disputed question;
- featured fan theory;
- featured fan art;
- real membership/community milestones.

Never manufacture activity or fake counts. Hide zero/empty states rather than invent momentum.

## Priority 9 — Build a content loop, not a content calendar

The preferred growth loop is:

**Short-form hook → question → comments/votes → response content → deeper site/Patreon discussion → reading → new question.**

Community reactions should create the next piece of content whenever possible.

## Priority 10 — Measurement

Measure movement through the funnel, not vanity views alone.

Track where technically feasible:

- House Test starts and completions;
- House result distribution;
- share attempts;
- click-through to The Banner;
- click-through to Patreon;
- free member growth;
- paid conversion;
- Moot participation;
- returning participants;
- comments / theory submissions;
- social retention and chose-to-view signals when available from the platform.

No personal data should be collected merely for growth analytics when anonymous aggregation is sufficient.

## Implementation order

### Phase A — Identity loop (build first)
1. House Test.
2. Anonymous House-result aggregation in Supabase.
3. Result sharing.
4. Route result into The Banner / selected House identity.
5. Free Patreon CTA.

### Phase B — Ritual loop
1. Reframe one Moot slot as recurring **The Ledger Question**.
2. Add archive/history of prior questions and outcomes.
3. Feed social-video CTAs into the current question.

### Phase C — Recognition loop
1. Public House-distribution module once sample size is meaningful.
2. Featured theories/art/comments.
3. Founding Reader program once paid community mechanics are ready.

### Phase D — Content engine
1. Recurring Sela/Tomas/Alaine character formats.
2. Nine/Twelve evidence-conflict format.
3. Community-response videos.
4. Patreon creator notes tied to each public content cycle.

## Guardrails for Claude and future agents

1. Read this file before community, growth, Patreon, social funnel or fandom work.
2. Treat it as implementation strategy, **not canon**.
3. `canon/SOURCE_REGISTRY.md` and current canon files always win on fictional facts.
4. Never invent fan counts, patrons, votes, testimonials or momentum.
5. Never let community voting determine protected canon, central plot, character fates or protected mysteries.
6. Preserve the moral defensibility of all ten Houses.
7. Prefer strengthening existing Banner/Moot/Scriptorium infrastructure before adding new platforms.
8. Every growth feature should answer: does this create **identity, participation, habit, or belonging**? If not, challenge whether it should exist.
9. The immediate product priority is **House Test → Ledger Question → free Patreon membership**.
10. When implementation and this document diverge, update this document or explicitly record the deliberate deviation so future Claude sessions do not reconstruct strategy from chat history.
