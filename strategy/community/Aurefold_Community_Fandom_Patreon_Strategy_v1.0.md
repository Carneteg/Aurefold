# Aurefold — Community, Fandom & Patreon Strategy v1.0

**Status:** PRODUCT / COMMUNITY STRATEGY — NON-CANON

**Owner directive:** This file is governing operational strategy for community growth, fandom, social content and Patreon conversion. It does not create or modify story canon. Canon and source authority remain governed by `canon/SOURCE_REGISTRY.md` and the Constitution/Ledger stack.

## Strategic objective

Do not treat Aurefold primarily as a book to advertise. Build Aurefold into a participatory identity system in which readers discover the world, choose a philosophical affiliation, debate defensible moral questions, return for recurring rituals, form attachments to characters, and only then choose to support the project financially.

The desired funnel is:

**Discovery → Identity → Participation → Community → Habit → Free membership → Paid support**

A practical reader journey should look like:

**I saw a short → I took the House test → I identify with a House → I argued with another House → I started reading → I joined free → I return for the next question → I choose to support Aurefold.**

## Priority 1 — The House Test as acquisition engine

The existing House Quiz should evolve from a light personality quiz into a 10–15 question moral-dilemma test.

Rules:
- Questions should be difficult choices between defensible values, not superficial personality prompts.
- No House may be treated as the morally correct answer.
- Questions should surface tensions such as truth vs stability, loyalty vs conscience, progress vs human cost, knowledge vs access, courage vs prudence, unity vs dissent, faith vs evidence, honor vs consequence, record vs mercy, future vs present sacrifice.
- Results should identify one of the ten Great Houses.
- Result pages should explain both the attraction and the cost of that House philosophy.
- Results should be shareable.
- Aggregate House distribution should be stored and later surfaced publicly without exposing personal data.
- The test is community/product material, not canon.

Strategic aim: make people say **“I’m Blackthorn”**, **“I’m Whitehart”**, etc. House identity should become a repeatable fandom shorthand.

## Priority 2 — The Ledger Question

Create a recurring Aurefold ritual called **The Ledger Question**.

Format:
- One morally difficult question at a time.
- Usually two defensible choices.
- No official correct answer.
- Publish on the website, TikTok, Shorts, Reels and Patreon.
- Use the same core question across channels so discussion compounds rather than fragments.
- Results and strongest arguments become content for the next cycle.

The Ledger Question should feel native to Aurefold’s central moral concern: **How should humanity live together?**

It must never be used to let fans vote on protected canon, central plot outcomes, mysteries, character fates or authorial truth.

## Priority 3 — “Nine / Twelve” as a repeatable content format

Use conflicting accounts as a social format:
- two witnesses;
- two maps;
- two archives;
- two House interpretations;
- two defensible versions of the same event.

End with a question such as **“Who do you believe?”** or **“Which record would you keep?”**

This format should reinforce Aurefold’s contested-history identity without objectively resolving protected mysteries.

## Priority 4 — Build fandom around people before encyclopedic lore

Create recurring character-led content for Sela, Tomas, Alaine and other high-value characters.

Examples of repeatable character formats:
- “Sela has no patience for…”
- “Tomas would technically object…”
- “Alaine judges…”
- short moral choices framed through how a character would react;
- recognizable verbal and visual fingerprints.

Goal: readers should develop favourite characters before they can recite institutional lore.

## Priority 5 — Patreon free membership before paid conversion

Patreon should act as the low-friction bridge between social discovery and supporter conversion.

The first Patreon ask should generally be **Follow / Join free**, not **Pay now**.

Free member value may include:
- The Ledger Question;
- production updates;
- artwork;
- selected behind-the-scenes material;
- creator notes;
- social-video follow-ups;
- announcements.

Paid support should monetize closeness, access and process — not hold the main story hostage.

## Priority 6 — Paid Patreon proposition

Keep the initial tier system simple. Suggested launch structure:
- approximately €4;
- approximately €8;
- approximately €15.

Potential benefits:
- annotated chapters;
- creator commentary;
- worldbuilding process;
- deleted/alternate material clearly labeled **NON-CANON**;
- early artwork/video access;
- monthly creator Q&A;
- deeper discussion rooms;
- production notes and drafts where appropriate.

Hard rule: payment does not purchase canon authority, protected mystery answers, character survival, House superiority or control of the main plot.

## Priority 7 — Community spaces

Use the existing Aurefold website community layer and Patreon community tools before opening a large Discord.

Avoid creating an empty multi-channel Discord simply because fandom projects are expected to have one. A small active room is stronger than a dead server.

Suggested discussion layers:
- spoiler-free;
- Book One spoilers;
- theories;
- House discussion;
- creator/community questions.

Open a broader Discord only after recurring participation demonstrates that the community needs a larger persistent space.

## Priority 8 — Founding Readers

Create a real-world community recognition layer for early supporters.

Potential implementation:
- first 100 or 250 qualifying supporters receive a permanent **Founding Reader** digital badge;
- badge may appear in profile/community presentation;
- it is a fandom/community marker only.

Hard rule: Founding Reader status does not make a supporter canonical nobility, a House officer, a historical figure, or otherwise part of story canon.

## Priority 9 — Make community activity visible

Over time, surface aggregate participation on aurefold.com:
- House distribution;
- current Ledger Question result;
- most disputed question;
- selected community arguments;
- fan art;
- theory activity;
- participation milestones.

Privacy principle: show aggregate or deliberately public community information, not hidden personal behaviour.

## Priority 10 — Build a content loop, not a content calendar

Preferred loop:

**Short-form video → question → comments/votes → community argument → response video/post → deeper website/Patreon discussion → reading → next question**

Community response should generate the next round of content whenever possible.

## Measurement

Do not optimize on raw views alone.

Track the funnel:
- short view / hold rate;
- click-through to Aurefold;
- House Test starts;
- House Test completions;
- House result shares;
- Ledger Question votes/comments;
- free Patreon joins;
- return participation;
- reader conversion;
- paid Patreon conversion;
- retention of supporters.

The key community metric is not audience size in isolation. It is the number of people who **return voluntarily and participate again**.

## Canon safety

All community mechanics are downstream of canon and must preserve these principles:
- no objectively correct House;
- no fan vote can rewrite locked canon;
- no engagement mechanic can solve protected mysteries;
- no quiz result may imply metaphysical truth;
- no Patreon tier may purchase canon power;
- community lore/content must be marked as canon, proposal, non-canon, or community material according to the project’s authority system.
