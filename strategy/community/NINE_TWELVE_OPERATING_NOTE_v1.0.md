# Aurefold — NINE / TWELVE Operating Note v1.0

**Status:** NON-CANON COMMUNITY / SOCIAL OPERATIONS

## Governing format

Use:
`marketing/social/formats/NINE_TWELVE_SOCIAL_FORMAT_v1.0.md`

The NINE / TWELVE format is the approved repeatable conflicting-record social mechanic for Aurefold.

It exists to turn Aurefold's contested-history principle into a fan-facing acquisition and discussion format without solving protected mysteries or converting ambiguity into marketing certainty.

## Original Gate anchor

The Gate launch is canon-sensitive and must always preserve:
- inward-opening Gate;
- no objectively established first aggressor;
- nine bodies and twelve missing as separate incompatible records;
- no exact fifteen-minute claim;
- no objective magical explanation;
- no resolution of Col or any other protected uncertainty.

## Production loop

For each approved NINE / TWELVE story:
1. verify source authority/status;
2. identify Record A and exactly what it measures;
3. identify Record B and exactly what it measures;
4. identify the unresolved gap;
5. create one short-form hook and one reader decision question;
6. add a unique `?src=ninetwelve-*` marker;
7. route to the current Ledger Question or another explicitly approved community action;
8. review attributed entry-to-vote behavior in Community Manager.

## Source status discipline

- Canon-derived pieces may state canon facts but must preserve canon ambiguity.
- Proposal-derived pieces must be labeled proposal-derived and may not be presented as established world history.
- Community-created examples must be labeled community/non-canon.
- Poll outcomes never become canon.

## Editorial test

Do not publish a NINE / TWELVE piece unless both sides can be stated in a way an intelligent reader could defend.

Do not manufacture false balance. If the source evidence strongly supports one factual claim and weakens another, say so. The format is about competing records and methods, not pretending all claims are equally credible.

## Core success metric

The format is successful when a viewer becomes a participant and later returns: attributed social entry → Ledger vote → next Ledger return.

Raw view count alone is not success.
