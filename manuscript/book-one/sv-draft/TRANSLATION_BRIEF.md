# AUREFOLD Book One — Swedish translation brief (governing)

## Task
Translate the assigned sections of *The Bell of Silence* (English Master v1.7) into literary Swedish. This is a published edition, not a gist: every sentence carries, register matters, and the author's voice fingerprints must survive.

## Register
Mature literary epic fantasy. Materially grounded, unsentimental, precise. Swedish should read as written-in-Swedish prose — natural syntax, no anglicisms, no translationese. Prefer concrete Swedish over Latinate abstraction. Preserve sentence rhythm variation: where the English is clipped, be clipped; where it runs, run.

## Voice fingerprints (must survive translation)
- Sela: practical, bodily, earthy; dry institutional skepticism; never poetic about herself.
- Tomas: exact, corrective, pedantic, extremely dry; unintentionally funny through precision. His corrections must stay grammatically fussy in Swedish.
- Wren: evidence, method, observation vs inference; explicit uncertainty.
- Fen: omission, counting, names; tired, capable, fully human.
- Alaine: hierarchy, judgment, ritual competence; sharp irony, satirical condescension.
- Perrin: records, custody, what survives; professional charm.
- Corven: warm, expansive, comfortable with large ideas.

## Glossary (binding)
- All character names: unchanged (Sela, Tomas, Wren, Fen, Alaine, Perrin, Harl, Ivet, Merta, Col, Orla, Corven, Roderick, Signe, Joric, Tam, Ansel, Hessa Morn, Jeren Tesk, Aren Lethren, Elya, Brem, Sinnet, ...).

- Title of the book: keep "The Bell of Silence" wherever it appears as title.

## Locked lines
- The final line of the book, "She went east.", must be exactly: **"Hon gick österut."**
- Do not resolve ambiguities the English preserves. If the English withholds, the Swedish withholds.

## Mechanics
- Input: markdown files, one section each: `## <heading>` followed by prose paragraphs.
- Output: SAME markdown structure. Translate the heading too ("Chapter One" → "Kapitel ett"; "Prologue" → "Prolog"; "Interlude" → "Interlud"; chapter titles translated in the same register).
- Preserve paragraph breaks exactly — same number of paragraphs in = out.
- Preserve italics (*...*) and em-dashes. Swedish quotation style: use quotation marks as in the source (curly quotes), do not switch to talstreck.
- Numbers, measures: translate naturally (forty-three pages → fyrtiotre sidor).
- Do NOT summarize, do NOT skip sentences, do NOT add anything.
