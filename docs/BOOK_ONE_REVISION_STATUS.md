# Book One Revision Status

## Current status

**The Bell of Silence — English Master v1.6** is the current governing working manuscript for Book One.

It is **not** a finished, frozen, publication-locked, or final manuscript.

`governing` means that v1.6 is the baseline used for current continuity checks and future revision. It does not mean that the novel is complete.

## Revision remains open

Book One remains open to substantial improvement, including:

- structural revision
- pacing and scene-value revision
- character depth, magnetism and differentiation
- relationship architecture
- dramatic escalation and consequence
- commercial readability and attachment
- House/world integration
- prose and dialogue revision

The current manuscript should therefore be treated as an active literary work in progress whose weaknesses may require more than micro-editing.

## What is actually locked

Only explicitly adopted canon constraints are immutable. This includes the locked Book One endpoint controls such as the Gate opening inward, no objectively established first attacker at the Gate, the separate nine-bodies/twelve-missing counts, Col remaining unconfirmed, Sela's Ledger page remaining blank, Sela leaving east with Harl's knife, and the Bell remaining objectively unexplained.

Revision may rebuild material around those locks, but may not contradict or explain them away.

## Database semantics

`lore_books.state = governing` identifies the manuscript version currently used as the operational baseline.

`lore_books.revision_status = active_revision` records the separate fact that the manuscript is still under active developmental revision.

A manuscript must never be described as frozen or finished merely because it is the governing baseline.
