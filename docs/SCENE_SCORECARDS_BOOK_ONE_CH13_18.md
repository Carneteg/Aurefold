## Scene-level conclusions

### Chapter 13 - The Corrected Entry

Merta refuses to sell Perrin a humanizing childhood story, corrects his wording to observed acts and proves that Whitehart copied Col's winter-year as completed age. Tomas records seventeen beside the visible error. The correction makes the harmed person more exact without changing the red judgment.

Protected remainder: correction is not total truth. Age evidence cannot recreate the burned trial, decide Col's fate or reopen the judgment.

### Chapter 14 - Not That Way

Sela's exact wrong-thaw warning immediately becomes both sacred prohibition and evidence that she is merely frightened. Deposits, roof slate and wages make every version economically consequential. She cannot create a road that remains only a road.

Protected remainder: material route knowledge is not prophecy. No method becomes omniscient, and the future failure is not known here.

### Chapter 15 - Nine Fares

Harl offers Sela six false words that would make him turn. She refuses the useful lie and gives only ground, fear and uncertainty. Harl believes her, establishes practical consent and still takes nine fares by the inspected neck.

Protected remainder: the outcome is not yet known. Harl's choice is defensible on incomplete ground; Sela reports no voice and possesses no supernatural certainty.

### Chapter 16 - Four Accounts of a Rope

Roderick saves exposed survivors and Brem while losing the use of his shoulder. Four bounded accounts preserve incompatible positions, memories and wording. The phrase that he went first travels; the retreat order that prevented a second fall does not.

Protected remainder: no single heroic version owns the event. No witness sees the whole slope, and a successful rescue does not make its example harmless.

### Chapter 17 - What Harl Became

Fen prepares Harl's body while the event becomes songs, hearings and a Chronicle line. Perrin's sentence contains no false assertion, yet its order makes sacred defiance precede wet ground. The detailed source packet survives behind the line most people will hear.

Protected remainder: Harl cannot correct the living. Public wording, source evidence and the event itself remain distinct.

### Chapter 18 - The Rule of Six Words

Sela's brave defense prevents total silence but lets Alaine turn friendship into exclusive access: only the Called may speak to Fen. Fen uses the one refusal left to him and asks Sela never to say his name again. She obeys without improving his answer.

Protected remainder: exclusive privilege is not repair. Fen's standing is not restored, and Sela's authority does not own his name or identity.
