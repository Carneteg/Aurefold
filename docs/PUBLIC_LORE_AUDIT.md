## Protected spoiler boundary

The Book One Canon Appendix v1.3 classifies as full spoilers: Sela's final blank page, Harl's knife in the ending, Ivet's death, exact Gate counts, Wren's final divided report, Tomas surrendering the Ledger key and the Bell's final sounding.

These may exist in author-only database tables but must not enter public site views merely because the database knows them.

## Contested-history rule

Public history must never be generated by flattening internal `lore_claims` into one omniscient statement.

Example already modeled internally:

- Whitehart supports nine received bodies after the Gate.
- Valley checks support twelve missing.
- The measurements remain distinct.

Likewise, Wren's identification of Jeren Tesk's first observable bolt at Lower Field is a bounded observation and must never be rendered as an objective answer to who began the violence.

## Release rule

A fact reaches the public site only when all three are true:

1. its controlling source permits the fact;
2. its spoiler/public classification permits release;
3. it is exposed through an explicit public projection/view.

Existing HTML is a presentation fallback, not a higher canon authority than the Constitution or current control files.
