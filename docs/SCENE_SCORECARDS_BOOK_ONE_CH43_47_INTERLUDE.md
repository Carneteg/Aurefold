## Scene-level conclusions

### Interlude - The Question in the Margin

The apparent completeness of Whitehart's return depends on the deliberate destruction of the trial observations behind its judgments. Wren prevents the Chronicle from converting House recognition into external confirmation and replaces a loaded voice question with a bounded commission about practice, testimony, consequences, unfinished trials, provenance and access. Perrin nevertheless carries the more memorable question forward.

Protected remainder: the parties-versus-Houses wording anomaly is non-probative.

### Chapter 43 - The Keeper

Fen preserves paired names among the Gate dead, receives Wilda's account of carrying Col across the shoulder and gives that account to Merta after naming his own prior complicity. He refuses to exchange the account for recognition and returns to the wall. Merta gains a choice and loses a fixed place for grief.

Protected remainder: Wilda last saw Col alive and walking out of sight. The account does not establish survival or death.

### Chapter 44 - The Divided Report

Wren separates observation, inference, use and harm, keeps nine received bodies distinct from twelve valley absences, and withholds the Fen source key even though doing so costs the report certified completeness. The scene makes ethical incompleteness an evidentiary choice rather than a gap to conceal.

Protected remainder: no reconciled Gate count, single first aggressor or exclusive mechanical cause is established.

### Chapter 45 - That's How You Know

Nella's grain is the result of prior planning, credit and sixteen loaded travel days rather than a response summoned by the Gate. Her transparent terms limit hidden coercion without removing dependency. Sela's direct denial of Corrin's fabricated line is converted into proof, and Tam's violence gives the false account new custody.

Protected remainder: the quotation remains false even when it becomes socially effective.

### Chapter 46 - The Blank Page

Tomas finishes the test without entering black or red, surrenders the Ledger key and accepts loss of office. The blank page limits Whitehart's ability to claim it judged Sela but does not classify or free her. Sela refuses a new test and leaves through a category the gate list never created.

Protected remainder: the blank page is not a positive judgment, and it cannot create canon.

### Chapter 47 - East

Alaine declines to call Sela back without converting that restraint into a blessing. Sela crosses by the unsupported third way, abandons force when the rock will not admit her, reaches the stream and hears the Bell sounding beyond its ordinary seven-stroke political count. She continues east through immediate material needs rather than revelation.

Protected remainder: Sela knows the Bell sounds. She does not know its cause, meaning or divine status, and Col remains unresolved.
