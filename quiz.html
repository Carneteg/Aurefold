Which house would you follow?

Ten Great Houses rule the Banner-lands, each a different wager about what people are for. Six questions. No wrong answers — only which oath you'd swear.

Ten banners, ten wagers: Blackthorn trusts intellect, Ashbourne courage, Whitehart faith, Stormrider unity, Ravenshade information, Ironvale innovation, Blackcrest the victor's history, Stonebear honor, Tidebreaker knowledge, and Phoenix the future. Six questions ask which of them you would follow.
