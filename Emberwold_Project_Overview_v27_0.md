# THE CORE

Emberwold is a long-form epic fantasy series in **mystic realism**. Magic remains nearly invisible, unreliable, and open to mundane explanation. The Ten Great Houses embody competing philosophies of leadership and power. The reader's governing question is:

> **Which house would I follow?**

The series is not structured as good against evil. Every Great House has produced real good and real victims. Ashen Crown stands outside this equivalence because its ideology permits dehumanization and purification.

The central long mystery is a forgotten eleventh house erased from an otherwise documented age. Its name, philosophy, seat, and fate remain unresolved.

---

# WORLD FRAME

- **Present:** 396 A.U., four years before the 400-year Jubilee.
- **Continent:** the Banner-lands.
- **Western / southern boundary:** the Sleeping Sea.
- **Eastern boundary:** the Silent Mountains.
- **Political center:** the Ash Fields and the seat known variously as Ederhamn, Elvestad, or Ederstad.
- **Historical method:** Chronicles, songs, fragments, marginal notes, and institutional silences disagree.

Series-level phrase:

> **The map ends where the truth begins.**

The definitive Book One dramatic use remains:

> The map ended.  
> She kept walking.
