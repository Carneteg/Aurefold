# Aurefold — NINE / TWELVE Social Format v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED NON-CANON MARKETING / COMMUNITY FORMAT  
**Purpose:** repeatable short-form social format for fandom acquisition, debate and return participation.  
**Authority:** this file does not create canon. It must remain downstream of the governing Aurefold canon/source stack and the current Book One governing manuscript.

## Why this format exists

Aurefold should not market itself by explaining its lore until the mystery has been flattened. The recurring **NINE / TWELVE** format instead shows how Aurefold thinks:

- two records can survive the same event;
- each may be defensible within its own method;
- the disagreement itself can be historically important;
- institutional certainty must not be manufactured simply because audiences prefer one clean number.

The format is named after the protected Book One outcome:

**Nine bodies. Twelve missing.**

Those are separate counts. They are not alternative estimates of one total and must never be reconciled into a single definitive number.

## Canon locks for the original Gate story

Any Gate-derived NINE / TWELVE post must preserve all of the following:

1. The Gate opens inward.
2. No objectively established first aggressor.
3. Nine bodies and twelve missing remain separate, incompatible records.
4. Do not imply that one record is secretly the true total.
5. Do not imply that Whitehart, the valley, Tomas, Roderick or any witness possesses the objective full sequence.
6. Do not use an exact fifteen-minute duration. The crush is under a quarter of an hour.
7. Do not turn the event into evidence of supernatural causation.
8. Do not use the format to solve Col's fate or any other protected uncertainty.

## Core creative rule

Every NINE / TWELVE piece contains **two defensible records and one unresolved gap**.

The audience is invited to decide what they would preserve, trust, question or investigate — not to discover a hidden authorial answer.

### Required structure

**Beat 1 — The contradiction**  
Show the disagreement immediately.

**Beat 2 — Record A**  
State exactly what this record counts, observes or claims.

**Beat 3 — Record B**  
State exactly what the second record counts, observes or claims.

**Beat 4 — Why both can survive**  
Explain the methodological difference without collapsing it.

**Beat 5 — The missing certainty**  
Name what neither record can establish.

**Beat 6 — The reader question**  
End with a decision prompt.

Preferred ending questions:
- `Which record would you keep?`
- `Which record would you trust first?`
- `What would you investigate next?`
- `When do two honest records become one political problem?`
- `Who gets to decide which account becomes history?`

Avoid `What really happened?` when the underlying canon requires permanent ambiguity.

# REPEATABLE NINE / TWELVE TEMPLATE

The format may be used beyond the Gate only when the source material genuinely supports competing records or interpretations.

## Template

**Title:** `[RECORD A] / [RECORD B]`

**Hook:**  
`Both records survived. They do not agree.`

**Record A:**
- provenance / observer;
- what it actually measures;
- what it omits;
- why a reasonable person might trust it.

**Record B:**
- provenance / observer;
- what it actually measures;
- what it omits;
- why a reasonable person might trust it.

**Unresolved gap:**
- state exactly what cannot be established.

**Reader prompt:**
- ask what to preserve, investigate or trust;
- never ask fans to determine locked canon by majority vote.

## Eligible future sources

Use only canon-safe or clearly labeled proposal/community material with correct status labels. Strong candidates include:
- conflicting witness sequence;
- official record vs household account;
- map vs traveler report;
- Blackcrest public wording vs private testimony;
- two House interpretations of a shared event;
- archival count vs material remains.

If the underlying source is PROPOSAL, the social piece must also say it is proposal-derived and must not present it as established world history.

# MODERATION RULES

- Reward reasoning, not simply the majority side.
- Do not correct a reader for choosing one record unless they misstate what the record actually measures.
- Correct canon errors calmly: nine bodies and twelve missing are separate counts.
- Do not hint that the creator secretly knows the definitive first aggressor and is withholding it.
- Do not manufacture reader quotes.
- Do not use poll results as canon evidence.
- Do not let House identity become factional harassment.
