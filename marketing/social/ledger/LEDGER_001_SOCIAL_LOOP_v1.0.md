**Status:** APPROVED NON-CANON marketing/community material  
**Campaign:** `ledger-001-incomplete-warning`  
**Live window:** 19–26 August 2026  
**Primary CTA:** `https://aurefold.com/vote.html`  
**Campaign objective:** turn social discovery into a vote, then a return visit for the next Ledger Question.

## Campaign principle

This campaign does not sell lore. It invites the reader into an argument.

The question is deliberately simple enough to understand without Aurefold knowledge and difficult enough that a decent person can defend either answer. Never frame one side as cruel, stupid, cowardly or obviously correct.

Every post should route to The Moot with a source marker:

# COMMUNITY MANAGEMENT RULES

- Like and reply to arguments from both sides.
- Never use the official account to declare which answer is wiser.
- Ask follow-up questions that expose trade-offs: `What would make you change your answer?`, `Who should carry the risk?`, `Does scale change your choice?`
- Do not reward hostility with amplification.
- If a reader produces a strong argument, ask permission before quoting them outside the original platform if attribution would identify them.
- Results are descriptive community history, not moral truth.
