## Second-angle change

The launch asked whether to publish a credible but incomplete warning when disclosure itself could cause deadly panic. Friday reframed the split around who should carry uncertainty.

Sunday changes one assumption: **the institution can reduce panic by publishing the warning together with clear protective instructions — but the evidence itself remains incomplete.**

This preserves two defensible positions:

- **Publish:** if secondary harm can be materially reduced, withholding a credible warning becomes harder to justify because people regain both information and a safer way to act on it.
- **Wait:** better communication does not make incomplete evidence more reliable; a calm, authoritative warning can still mobilize large numbers of people around something that may be false.

Neither position is presented as Aurefold's answer.
