## Changed assumption

The original question asks whether a credible but incomplete warning should be published when disclosure itself may cause deadly panic.

Sunday changes one consequence:

**Assume the institution can publish the warning together with an explicit statement that the evidence is incomplete — but it still cannot control what people do with that uncertainty.**

This removes the easy objection that publication must pretend certainty. It does **not** remove the risk of panic, nor the cost of withholding information.
