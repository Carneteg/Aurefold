## Changed assumption

Original dilemma: the treaty likely saves thousands but leaves a small border community under the rule of the side that abused them.

Sunday change: **the abandoned border community itself asks you to sign the treaty because renewed war is likely to reach them first.**

This changes who appears to be carrying the sacrifice without removing the underlying conflict. Signing may respect the community's stated choice and save thousands; refusing may still be defensible if consent given under threat cannot legitimize handing people back to their abusers.
