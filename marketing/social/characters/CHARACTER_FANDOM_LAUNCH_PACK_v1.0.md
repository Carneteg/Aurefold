# POST 1 — SELA HAS NO PATIENCE FOR “ACCEPTABLE LOSS”

**Primary goal:** attachment to Sela through practical moral intelligence.  
**Destination:** `https://aurefold.com/vote.html?src=character-sela-acceptable-loss`

## TikTok / Reels / Shorts — 20 sec

### Opening text

`SELA HAS NO PATIENCE FOR:`  
`“ACCEPTABLE LOSS.”`

### Voiceover

> Aurefold has institutions with excellent words for necessity. Sela tends to notice the part the words leave out: someone still has to lift the body, mend the gate, feed the animal, or explain the empty chair at supper. “Acceptable” is an interesting word when someone else carries the weight.

### End card

`WHO GETS TO CALL A COST ACCEPTABLE?`  
`AUREFOLD · SELA`

### Visual beats

1. Sela's red, callused hands tighten a rough cloth strip.
2. Formal text `ACCEPTABLE LOSS` appears on clean paper.
3. The paper slides aside to reveal worn wood, mending, work, an empty place at a plain table.
4. Return to Sela's hands doing useful work rather than posing.
5. End question.

### Caption

Sela's intelligence is not abstract. She notices who carries the thing everyone else has found a respectable word for.

That does not make her automatically right. Practical people can make brutal compromises too.

**Who gets to decide when a human cost is “acceptable”?**

What official phrase makes a real human cost sound suspiciously clean?

# POST 2 — TOMAS WOULD TECHNICALLY OBJECT

**Primary goal:** make Tomas recognizable, quotable in spirit, and debate-generating without inventing dialogue.  
**Destination:** `https://aurefold.com/vote.html?src=character-tomas-no-evidence`

## TikTok / Reels / Shorts — 20 sec

### Opening text

`“THERE IS NO EVIDENCE.”`

Beat.

`TOMAS WOULD TECHNICALLY OBJECT.`

### Voiceover

> No evidence? Or no evidence that permits the conclusion you want? Tomas is the kind of man who can make one missing clause feel unbearable. It is funny until you remember that institutions ruin people by deleting inconvenient distinctions all the time.

### End card

`WHEN DOES PEDANTRY BECOME PROTECTION?`  
`AUREFOLD · TOMAS`

### Visual beats

1. Clean sentence on paper: `THERE IS NO EVIDENCE.`
2. Charcoal-marked fingers enter frame and underline `NO`.
3. A second clause is written beneath: `THAT PERMITS THIS CONCLUSION.`
4. Tomas's precise notes fill margins while the main verdict remains blank.
5. End question.

### Caption

Tomas is often funny because he is not trying to be.

He cares about the distinction everyone else would prefer to skip.

Sometimes that is pedantry. Sometimes the missing distinction is the only thing between uncertainty and an official lie.

**Which distinction sounds petty until someone's life depends on it?**

The Ledger is open → https://aurefold.com/vote.html?src=character-tomas-no-evidence

### Pinned comment

Is refusing an unsupported conclusion courage, cowardice — or neither?

# POST 3 — ALAINE JUDGES PERFORMED CERTAINTY

**Primary goal:** create fascination with Alaine as perceptive, difficult authority rather than “wise elder.”  
**Destination:** `https://aurefold.com/vote.html?src=character-alaine-certainty`

## TikTok / Reels / Shorts — 20 sec

### Opening text

`“THERE CAN BE NO DOUBT.”`

Beat.

`ALAINE JUDGES.`

### Voiceover

> Alaine does not need a person to be wrong before she distrusts the performance. Certainty announced too early can be an attempt to make rank do the work of evidence. The uncomfortable part is that Alaine also knows how useful rank can be when a room is frightened and someone has to decide.

### End card

`WHEN DOES AUTHORITY BECOME PART OF THE EVIDENCE?`  
`AUREFOLD · ALAINE`

### Visual beats

1. Formal chamber; speaker unseen; phrase `THERE CAN BE NO DOUBT.`
2. Alaine's iron-white flat braid and walking stick; she remains motionless.
3. Close on expressive eyes rather than mystical lighting.
4. Hierarchical seating / ritual order appears as diagram lines around the speaker.
5. The lines become a question mark rather than a verdict.

### Caption

Alaine understands institutions well enough to see when ceremony is hiding panic.

She also understands institutions well enough to defend them when other people would burn the whole structure down.

That is why she is more interesting than a cynical elder who simply mocks everyone beneath her.

**When does confidence become evidence only because the confident person has rank?**

Join The Moot → https://aurefold.com/vote.html?src=character-alaine-certainty

### Pinned comment

Would you rather be ruled by someone who doubts openly, or someone who can make a room believe they do not?

# POST 4 — THREE PEOPLE, ONE WORD: NECESSARY

**Primary goal:** teach character differentiation without lore exposition.  
**Destination:** `https://aurefold.com/vote.html?src=character-three-necessary`

## Carousel / text video

**Card 1**  
`THE DECISION IS NECESSARY.`

**Card 2 — SELA**  
`Necessary for whom — and who is carrying it?`

**Card 3 — TOMAS**  
`Necessary is a conclusion. What premises support it?`

**Card 4 — ALAINE**  
`Who gains authority by making necessity sound impersonal?`

**Card 5**  
`SAME WORD. THREE DIFFERENT QUESTIONS.`

**Card 6**  
`WHICH QUESTION WOULD YOU ASK FIRST?`

### Caption

A character voice is not just vocabulary.

It is the question a person cannot help asking first.

Sela looks for the material cost. Tomas looks for the unsupported inference. Alaine looks for the power hidden in the framing.

None of them gets to be Aurefold's official answer.

Which question would you ask first?

# RESPONSE BANK

Use these to turn comments into conversation without declaring a correct reading.

## Sela replies
- `Who is carrying the cost in your version?`
- `Would your answer change if the practical burden fell on you personally?`
- `What does the elegant wording hide materially?`

## Tomas replies
- `What fact would permit the stronger conclusion?`
- `Which word in the claim is doing more work than the evidence?`
- `Are you rejecting the conclusion, or only the confidence level?`

## Alaine replies
- `Does your answer change if the institution is competent?`
- `When is hierarchy genuinely useful?`
- `What does the room lose if nobody is willing to perform certainty?`
