# AUREFOLD — Google Flow trailer prompt pack

Grounded in the governing sources: Lore Bible DNA, Book One *The Bell of Silence*
(English Master v1.7), the Character Appearance Bible stack, and the canon-logged
teaser framing recorded in the project status file ("ten seals — some say eleven").

Everything below is written to be pasted into **Google Flow** (Veo). Prompts are in
English because the model follows English best. Nothing here resolves a protected
mystery, and nothing invents lore.

---

## 0. How to use this pack

Flow generates roughly **8 seconds per clip**. A 60-second teaser is therefore about
**8 clips assembled on the Flow timeline**, not one generation.

Three Flow modes matter here:

| Mode | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Ingredients to Video** | Character shots. Upload a ratified character-art image as an ingredient so the same face recurs across clips. This is the single most important consistency lever. |
| **Frames to Video** | Controlled moves — supply a first frame (e.g. the map plate) and let the camera drift. |
| **Text to Video** | Landscape, texture and object shots where no specific face is needed. |

**Use your own assets as Ingredients.** The project already holds 78 ratified character
images and ten hand-painted house sigils, plus the hand-drawn chart (Plate I). Feeding
those in beats describing a face in words every time.

Workflow: generate each shot below separately → keep the best take → assemble in order →
add the voiceover and the title card last.

## 1. THE STYLE BLOCK (paste into every prompt)

Put this at the end of every single prompt. It is what keeps eight separate generations
looking like one film.

> **Style:** Grounded historical realism, late-medieval to early-Renaissance material
> culture. Shot on 35mm anamorphic, shallow depth of field, natural light only —
> daylight, overcast sky, oil lamp, hearth, candle. Muted earth palette: wool brown,
> ash grey, parchment cream, iron, moss green, a single warm ember note. Skin, cloth and
> stone show wear, weather and use. No gloss, no fantasy sheen, no colour grading toward
> teal-orange. Faces are ordinary, weathered and specific, never model-beautiful.
> Camera is patient: slow push, slow drift, or locked off. No whip pans, no speed ramps,
> no slow motion.
>
> **Never include:** magic, glowing objects, spells, sparks, floating debris, non-human
> creatures, elves, dragons, monsters, armour that looks mass-produced, gunpowder
> artillery, machinery, gears, chimneys, modern hairstyles, clean teeth, makeup,
> lens flares, text overlays, watermarks.

*Why the "never" list matters:* Canon Lock 030 — magic in Aurefold is never objectively
confirmed. A single glowing effect in a trailer contradicts the whole premise. Only human
civilizations are confirmed. Technology is roughly 40% Renaissance, 30% late medieval,
20% antiquity, 10% unique Aurefold innovation, with no industrialization.

## 2. SIXTY-SECOND TEASER — shot by shot

Each block is one complete Flow prompt. Append the Style Block to each.

### Shot 1 — The chart (Frames to Video; first frame = Plate I map)
> Extreme close-up of a hand-inked map on aged paper, lamplight raking across the surface
> so the paper grain and ink bleed are visible. The camera drifts slowly right across
> mountain hatching toward an unmarked edge of the sheet. A hand in a worn wool sleeve
> enters frame and rests a finger on the paper, then stops.
> **Audio:** paper grain, a low room tone, one distant wind gust. No music yet.

### Shot 2 — The high valley (Text to Video)
> Wide landscape at dawn: steep green-grey mountain valleys under fast low cloud, bare
> rock above the treeline, thin goat tracks cut into the slope. A small herd of goats
> moves across the middle distance. Wind moves the grass in long waves. The camera holds
> still and lets the weather do the work.
> **Audio:** high-altitude wind, goat bells, one bird call. Music enters: a single low
> sustained cello note.

### Shot 3 — Sela working (Ingredients to Video; ingredient = ratified Sela portrait)
> A lean young woman in a patched wool coat crouches on a wet slope, holding a goat's
> foreleg and examining a split hoof with complete practical attention. Her brown hair is
> braided for work, not for looks. Her hands are red and thickened from weather and use.
> Wind pushes loose hair across her mouth and she ignores it. She does not look at camera.
> **Audio:** wind, the animal shifting, cloth, her breath.

### Shot 4 — The listening room (Text to Video)
> Interior of a stone chamber cut into a cliff, opening onto a black valley through a low
> arch. Along the inner wall, wrapped bodies are seated upright in open niches, facing the
> opening. A small oil lamp with a copper hood burns on a stone ledge, smoking slightly.
> Low wooden stools stand on a worn stone floor. No throne, no raised seat. The camera
> pushes in slowly toward the lamp.
> **Audio:** the lamp hissing, wind at the arch, distant dripping water. Cello holds.

### Shot 5 — Tomas and the lamp (Ingredients to Video; ingredient = ratified Tomas portrait)
> Close-up: a narrow-faced man of about forty-five, dark hair greying unevenly at the
> temples, beard clipped close with obvious precision, trims a lamp wick with the point of
> a small knife. Charcoal marks the creases of his fingers. He moves the lamp a hand's
> width to the left. The flame leans harder. He looks at it with the irritation of a man
> whose categories have been disobeyed.
> **Audio:** the knife on the wick, the flame guttering, cloth, a stool creaking off-screen.

### Shot 6 — Alaine (Ingredients to Video; ingredient = ratified Alaine portrait)
> An old woman sits perfectly still in a stone chamber, both hands resting on the head of
> a walking stick. Her hair is iron-white, braided flat against her head. Her face has been
> pared thin by age without softening. Only her eyes move — large, alive, judging and
> amused at once. She does not speak. The camera holds on her for a long beat.
> **Audio:** near silence, the room tone, one distant wind gust. Cello adds a second note.

### Shot 7 — The seals (Text to Video, or Ingredients with the ten sigils)
> Ten wax seals on aged cord laid out in a row on a dark table, each pressed with a
> different heraldic device, lit from one side so the wax edges catch. The camera tracks
> slowly along the row. At the end of the row the table continues into shadow, and the
> space where an eleventh seal would sit is empty and unlit. Hold on the empty space.
> **Audio:** the music drops out entirely for two seconds. Room tone only.

### Shot 8 — East (Ingredients to Video; ingredient = ratified Sela portrait)
> Wide shot from behind: a lone figure with a coil of cloth over one shoulder walks away
> from camera along a rutted road, out of the mountain shadow into flat open country under
> an enormous pale sky. She does not look back. The road continues past the edge of frame.
> **Audio:** wind, footsteps on grit. A single struck bell tone, plain and unresonant, with
> no reverb tail and no swell.

### Shot 9 — Title card (make this in a graphics tool, not Flow)
> Black. The AUREFOLD wordmark. Beneath it: *The Bell of Silence — Book One.* Then:
> *Read it free at aurefold.com.*

## 3. VOICEOVER

Keep it sparse. Two lines carry the whole teaser. Record it dry, close, unhurried —
an archivist reading a record, not a movie announcer.

- Over shots 1–2: *"Four hundred years ago, ten houses swore one oath."*
- Over shot 7, into the silence: *"Ten seals. Some say eleven."*
- Over shot 8: *"The map ends where the truth begins."*

The middle line is the canon-logged teaser framing; the last is the series tagline.
Both are already established, which is why they are safe to lead with.

**Optional instead of VO on shots 5–6** — two lines of real dialogue from the book:
Alaine's *"Leave it."* and Sela's *"How long?"* Dry delivery, no score under them.

---

## 4. FIFTEEN-SECOND CUT (TikTok / Reels)

Shots **2 → 7 → 8**, in that order, with only the middle voiceover line and the bell tone.
Vertical: re-generate shots 2 and 8 with "vertical 9:16 framing, subject small in frame,
sky and road dominant" added before the Style Block. Do not crop the 16:9 versions —
regenerate, or the composition dies.

## 5. GUARDRAILS — what a trailer must never do

These follow from locked canon and the spoiler-safe rule for public material.

1. **Never explain the Bell.** It may be heard. It is never shown ringing with a visible
   cause, and never given a supernatural treatment.
2. **Never show the Gate sequence or its outcome.**
3. **Never resolve the erased Eleventh House.** The empty place in shot 7 is the whole
   point: a gap, never an answer.
4. **Never show magic, prophecy, or anything that reads as divine confirmation.** Whitehart
   exists to *test* those who claim to hear. A trailer that implies Sela truly hears
   something breaks the premise the series is built on.
5. **Never show a named character not covered by ratified art**, and never invent a place
   name, banner or event for the sake of a shot.
6. **Never imply which house is right.** No house is the moral answer.
7. **Never state a follower or member count**, and never fabricate review quotes or laurels.

## 6. If a generation comes out wrong

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Faces drift between clips | Use Ingredients with the ratified portrait; do not describe the face in words as well — let the image lead. |
| Looks like generic fantasy | The Style Block is missing or truncated. Re-paste it in full; the "never include" list does most of the work. |
| Too clean, too pretty | Add: "clothing worn at cuffs and hems, mud on boots, chapped skin, uneven light." |
| Motion too busy | Add: "locked-off camera, no camera movement." Flow over-moves by default. |
| Wrong era creeping in | Add the specific object you want in shot — an oil lamp, a wax seal, a wool coat. Concrete objects anchor the period faster than adjectives. |
