# THE TEN GREAT HOUSES OF THE TENFOLD COMPACT

Ten houses swore the Tenfold Compact, and each is a different wager about what
people are for. None is the villain of the others' story. Every house holds a
virtue a decent person could live by — and every virtue asks someone to pay for
it. The house is the question, not the answer.

---

## Blackthorn — **Intellect**
> *"What should the most capable mind be permitted to decide for everyone else?"*

## Ashbourne — **Courage**
> *"When does bravery protect others, and when does it demand that others pay?"*

## Whitehart — **Faith**
> *"Can a community survive without shared meaning, and what happens when meaning requires certainty?"*

## Stormrider — **Unity**
> *"How much freedom must be bound together to keep roads, people, and obligations from breaking?"*

## Ravenshade — **Information**
> *"Who is safer when information moves freely, and who becomes exposed?"*

## Ironvale — **Innovation**
> *"Who benefits first from invention, and who is made obsolete or vulnerable?"*

## Blackcrest — **The Victor's History**
> *"Who decides which surviving account becomes usable history?"*

## Stonebear — **Honor**
> *"Can a public code restrain power without turning human complexity into shame?"*

## Tidebreaker — **Knowledge**
> *"When does preserving knowledge become control over those who produced it?"*

## Phoenix — **The Future**
> *"What may the present sacrifice for a future it cannot verify?"*

---

## The Eleventh House — **[OMITTED]**

There are eleven places in the Hall of Eleven and ten recognized voices. The
eleventh seat is neither named nor explained here. Its philosophy is
**unconfirmed**, and it stays that way: no single relic, chronicle, or testimony
can be presented as the answer to it. What the archive keeps of the eleventh is
its silence — and that silence is deliberate, not a gap to be filled.

---

### Developer & Canon Guardrails — DO NOT REMOVE

1. **No Magic:** no house is granted confirmed supernatural power; ideals are civic and human.
2. **No Good vs. Evil:** every house motivation stays grounded, complex, and defensible from its own perspective.
3. **The Eleventh stays unsolved:** never name it, never confirm its philosophy, never let one source resolve it.
