# ⚠️ CREATOR-ONLY — PRINCIPAL CHARACTERS OF BOOK ONE (FULL SPOILERS · DO NOT PUBLISH)

Each figure wants something ordinary and human, carries a contradiction they
cannot resolve cleanly, and ends the book changed but unfinished. No one here is
a villain; no one is vindicated.

---

## Sela — *Goatherd of the Light Heights*
- **Desire:** To keep her ordinary life and her family obligations.
- **Contradiction:** Her refusal to provide a sacred message becomes the very "miracle" others weaponize.
- **End state:** Leaves east bearing Harl's knife; her Ledger page is left blank.

## Tomas — *Tester of Whitehart*
- **Desire:** Exact procedure, to protect people from arbitrary rule.
- **Contradiction:** His unclosed mercy, years ago, left Fen legally erased.
- **End state:** Remains at Whitehart with an entered absence.

## Alaine — *Mother of Whitehart*
- **Desire:** To keep her community from collapsing.
- **Contradiction:** Her genuine love for Sela cannot be separated from her willingness to manipulate Sela's uncertainty to save the House.
- **End state:** Remains in Whitehart — loving, but compromised.

## Fen — *Keeper of the North Wall (the Unwritten)*
- **Desire:** To exist as a person rather than a legal classification.
- **Contradiction:** He buys back temporary social visibility by selling secrets to Perrin.
- **End state:** Chooses his own boundaries; refuses to monetize Wilda's secret.

## Perrin — *Blackcrest Herald*
- **Desire:** To preserve the ordinary lives history erases.
- **Contradiction:** Care and political extraction occupy the same chair in him.
- **End state:** Departs Whitehart with his incomplete record.

## Wren — *Crown-watch Investigator*
- **Desire:** Precise, objective evidence.
- **Contradiction:** A technically perfect investigation can still lack moral permission.
- **End state:** Withholds key source documents to prevent personal harm.

## Harl — *Kin to Sela; road expert*
- **Desire:** Practical survival over heroic stories.
- **Contradiction:** His refusal of heroic legend makes him the ultimate hero-legend after his death.
- **End state:** Dead — permanently. His knife and his skills carry on through Sela and Tam.

## Tam — *Harl's son*
- **Desire:** To protect his father's memory.
- **Contradiction:** His fury over simplified stories drives him to a brutal act of violence that creates another simplified story.
- **End state:** A long-term series pillar, carrying the consequences of violence.

## Merta — *Mother of Col*
- **Desire:** To know her condemned son's fate.
- **Contradiction:** She must accept a partial, morally messy truth instead of a clean, comforting answer.
- **End state:** Considers crossing the eastern pass, but turns back.

## Wilda — *Whitehart Keeper*
- **Desire:** To reconcile her past mercy with her institutional duty.
- **Contradiction:** She saved Col from ritual execution sixteen winters ago but forced his mother to wait in agonizing silence to protect the House rule.
- **End state:** Morally accountable; privately transfers her secret to Fen, because his Unwritten voice has no legal standing to destroy her.

### Developer & Canon Guardrails — DO NOT REMOVE

1. **No Magic:** no character has confirmed supernatural power; Sela's experiences stay framed as ambiguous directions or environmental impressions (wind, thermal stress, human expectation).
2. **Permanent Death:** Harl is permanently deceased — no resurrection, no spirit-speaking.
3. **No Good vs. Evil:** no house or person is a generic villain; every institutional motive stays grounded, complex, and defensible from its own perspective.
4. **Protected uncertainty:** Col's fate is never stated or resolved; Sela's Ledger page stays blank; the eleventh seat is never explained.
