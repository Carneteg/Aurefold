# HOUSE STORMRIDER: THE ROADS OF UNITY

- **Ideal:** Unity.
- **The Foundational Question:** *"At what cost do separate peoples build a single road, and who pays the toll of connection?"*
- **The Lived Reality:**
  - ***The Good:*** Stormrider is the Great House of Unity, bridging isolated valleys, maintaining fragile trade infrastructure, and providing physical connection across the continental frontiers.
  - ***The Victim:*** Under the weight of keeping the roads open, Stormrider demands that local variations, regional laws, and private boundaries surrender to the movement of the Compact's shared traffic.
