## Brem

**Established / now text-entered visual signature:** Twelve; hands not yet proportioned to the rest of him; still folds easily under the low family table; one stubborn lock of hair at the crown resists smoothing.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Brem still had the untidy proportions of twelve: hands arriving before the rest of him, long enough to make a piece of bread look smaller while his body could still fold under a low table without complaint. One lock of hair at the crown refused water, sleep, and his mother's palm with equal success. He looked most like himself when an interruption reached his face before the words did.

**Recognition motifs:** oversized adolescent hands; low-table posture; stubborn crown lock; interruption visible before speech.

**Guardrail:** Keep him twelve. Do not harden his appearance because later events injure him or because he belongs to Sela's family.

## Sinnet

**Established / now text-entered visual signature:** Bread-board worker; apron flour-grey at one hip; one thumbnail split down the middle and holding a permanent white line.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Sinnet wore bread work rather than decoration. Flour had greyed one hip of her apron where the same hand had been wiped for years, and a split thumbnail kept a white seam no washing quite removed. Her hands could insult, portion, and care in the same motion, which was part of why Fen noticed when one of those motions stopped.

**Recognition motifs:** flour-grey apron hip; split white-lined thumbnail; bread knife/heel; work-hardened repetitive gestures.

**Guardrail:** Do not sentimentalize her care for Fen. Habit and affection coexist with her participation in the institution that erases him.

## Hessa Morn

**Established / now text-entered visual signature:** Western-plain migrant grandmother; iron-bound shoeing chest; when the cart is threatened she lays one hand flat on the chest lid and pushes her chin forward slightly.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Hessa made possession physical. Whenever somebody with a board or order began speaking about the cart, one hand settled flat on the iron-bound chest and her chin moved forward by a fraction. She did not need a larger gesture. The body said the thing the paperwork kept trying to translate: this moved with me because it is mine.

**Recognition motifs:** palm on iron-bound chest; fractional chin-forward refusal; cart/grandchildren/goat clustered around her material life.

**Guardrail:** Do not turn her into a generic indomitable grandmother. Her resistance has practical costs and can obstruct systems that are also saving lives.

## Dova Sen

**Established / now text-entered visual signature:** Lower-road healer; sleeves rolled past the elbow despite cold; strip of clean linen looped twice around left wrist before needed.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Dova looked prepared one action ahead of the injury in front of her. Even in the cold her sleeves stayed above the elbow, and clean linen often travelled twice around her left wrist until a hand, splint, or binding needed it. The habit made her easy to find in a treatment row: not because she moved dramatically, but because readiness had become visible on her body.

**Recognition motifs:** rolled sleeves; linen loop on left wrist; kneeling beside injuries; pre-positioned practical materials.

**Guardrail:** Keep medicine naturalistic. No mystical intuition, impossible diagnosis, or healer aura.

## Daven

**Established / now text-entered visual signature:** Older novice at the wicket; novice grey arranged with cuffs turned to equal width and belt knot centered.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Daven wore novice grey as though disorder could be corrected by equal measures. His cuffs were turned to the same width, the belt knot centered, and nothing in the arrangement admitted that a rule might arrive crooked. To Fen, that neatness carried memory: they had once slept bed to bed, and one of them had learned how to fit the institution better than the other.

**Recognition motifs:** equal cuffs; centered knot; carefully arranged novice grey; wicket list/bar.

**Guardrail:** Neatness is not villainy. Daven is capable of memory, discomfort, and humane action even while remaining institutionally legible.
