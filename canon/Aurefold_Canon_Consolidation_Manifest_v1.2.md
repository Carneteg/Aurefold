## 6. Book One character/voice/rhythm implementation control

`Aurefold_Book_One_Character_Voice_and_Rhythm_Canon_v1.0.docx` is **ACTIVE / BINDING** as governing subordinate canon. Its principal binding implementation effects are:

- character before interpretation; no major character may function chiefly as an exposition vessel;
- Sela must retain private agency, concrete wants, bodily reality and dry institutional skepticism;
- major POV cognition and verbal fingerprints must be independently recognizable;
- exposition is compressed before new plot is added where meaning is already shown;
- major harm produces lived aftermath and durable human consequence;
- ordinary life, humor, work and low-intensity reality provide contrast with crisis;
- chapter-ending rhetoric must vary rather than default to aphoristic closure;
- continuity corrections require proof against governing sources;
- Fen must be physically established before speaking in the Ledger-room sequence in the next manuscript revision;
- the final movement is protected against unnecessary explanatory or spectacle expansion;
- each Great House requires lived social embodiment, internal dissent and material-cultural expression;
- each Great House requires official, common and outsider/hostile verbal layers, but individual phrases remain non-canon until separately adopted;
- Whitehart must visibly create genuine human good as well as genuine victims;
- Living Archive material remains non-canon unless separately ratified.

These directives govern implementation. They do not create new story facts or a Canon Lock #086.

## 7. Epistemic and provenance controls

- Observation, testimony, record, rumor, inference, legal effect, moral causation and public wording must remain distinguishable.
- A proposition may exist as a question, allegation or belief without being objectively true.
- Unknown timing remains unknown. Later knowledge, including `book_end` knowledge, must not leak backward into earlier scenes.
- Information transfer is not proof and is not identical to belief.
- Working propositions may remain `working/unresolved`; they cannot create a Canon Lock or ratify canon.
- Protected ambiguities may be strongly believed by a character but may not be represented as objectively solved knowledge.
- Claims derived from a changed manuscript section must be re-reviewed against the new body hash.
