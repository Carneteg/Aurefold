## Expansion Rule

A character should become recognizable from a few repeated human details, not from a dossier paragraph. First meaningful appearance may carry 1–2 visual anchors. Later scenes should reinterpret those anchors through POV, work, injury, status, or changed circumstance.

## Harl

**Established visual signature:** Heavy brows inherited by Tam; small knife worn at the front of his belt; road and roof work; boots wrapped in sacking at the seams in wet conditions.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Harl looked most himself while fixing something. The heavy brows that would later sit strangely on Tam made Harl seem sterner at rest than he usually behaved. A small knife lived at the front of his belt and moved from rope to wood to food without ceremony. His clothes belonged to weather and work rather than display; when the road turned wet he wrapped his boots at the seams instead of pretending good leather could argue with water. He could be on a roof with slate in one hand and nails in his mouth, or at a ladder shaving a wedge until the wobble stopped. The face mattered less than the impression of a man who noticed what would fail next.

**Recognition motifs:** Heavy brows; small knife; practical repairs; rope/wood; weather-beaten work gear.

**On-page use:** Let Harl's appearance arrive through competence and familiar tools. Do not romanticize him into a rugged archetype.

## Merta

**Established visual signature:** Fifty-two; uncovered hair in falling snow; carries Col's folded coat across both forearms; stillness oriented toward the path.

**Painterly prose target:**  
At fifty-two, Merta could stand in snow with her hair uncovered as if discomfort were merely another fact that had failed to answer her. The coat under her arm changed her more than any ornament could have. When she opened it, she did so with the practiced shake of someone who had folded and unfolded the same absence too many times. She held the garment across both forearms and faced the path as though a boy might still come down cold enough to need it.

**Recognition motifs:** Uncovered hair in snow; folded coat; both forearms supporting it; fixed attention toward the road.

**On-page use:** Her visual identity is grief made practical. Never aestheticize her sorrow or imply that waiting gives her supernatural knowledge.

## Wilda

**Established visual signature:** Unhurried even steps; controlled expression; often carries practical ritual objects or winter cloaks; later has a bandaged hand.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Wilda rarely looked hurried even when the work around her was. She moved with even steps and spent expression carefully, as though a face could become another form of public record if one was careless with it. Cloaks, poles, folded cloth, a bandaged hand — useful things tended to appear in her keeping. When emotion did break through, it was small: a mouth tightening, hands stopping, eyes closing once. The restraint made those movements more visible, not less.

**Recognition motifs:** Even pace; folded hands; carried cloth/cloaks; small breaks in facial control; bandaged hand.

**On-page use:** Do not make restraint synonymous with coldness. Her care is often visible through what she carries and does.

## Osric

**Established visual signature:** Writing board frequently in hand; mouth tightens when procedure fails; open hand receives Tomas's key.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Osric often seemed to organize himself around whatever had to be entered next. A writing board in his hands could become part of his posture, shoulders and attention narrowing toward the problem before him. His mouth tightened when language arrived too late for reality, and the gesture was small enough to miss unless one was watching for the moment procedure stopped being sufficient. Near the end, the same hands that had held boards and margins opened to receive Tomas's key.

**Recognition motifs:** Writing board; tightened mouth; held/open hands; procedural stillness.

**On-page use:** Avoid assigning generic priestly beauty or severity. His visual identity should grow from record-keeping and responsibility.

## Maren

**Established visual signature:** Compact build; wound-cart healer; moves among treatment rows; carries treatment sheets and clear vessels used for inspection.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Maren was compact enough to disappear behind taller carriers and reappear exactly where somebody had started bleeding, vomiting, or losing strength. The impression was less smallness than density: a body trained to occupy only the space care required. She moved with the hard economy of someone who had learned to make room around pain before anyone else thought to. Treatment sheets, vessels held against the light, cloth, salts, and names followed her through the camp.

**Recognition motifs:** Compact build; rapid economical movement; treatment sheets; clear vessels; proximity to wounds rather than authority.

**On-page use:** Keep her naturalistic and practical. No miraculous-healer aura.

## Halvard

**Established visual signature:** Nearly fifty; square through the middle; deaf enough on the left that he turns the other side toward quiet speakers; broken fittings in a leather roll and good ones loose in his coat; later sawdust in beard and split thumbnail.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Halvard was nearly fifty and square through the middle, built more like a workbench than a courtier. His left-side deafness shaped every conversation: he could turn the wrong shoulder toward a quiet speaker, then correct himself without apology and offer the right ear. Broken fittings travelled in a leather roll; good ones went loose into his coat as if successful objects needed less protection. By the time the crisis tightened he carried sawdust in his beard and a split thumbnail, which suited him better than ceremony ever could.

**Recognition motifs:** Square middle; right-ear turn; fittings in coat/roll; sawdust beard; split thumbnail; tactile testing.

**On-page use:** His deafness is a material fact, not shorthand for age, wisdom, or vulnerability. Let it affect staging naturally.

## Signe

**Established visual signature:** Arrives with cord, pegs, masons, and a board covered in lines; twenty years' planning experience.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Signe was easier to overlook than the things she carried. Cord, pegs, a marked board, masons at her shoulder — the eye went to the plan before it went to the planner. She had spent twenty years hearing bad planning called temporary and had the patience of someone who no longer mistook argument for surprise. What made her visually distinct was not grandeur but repeated measurement: she turned boards toward slopes, bent lines around hearths, and treated the ground as the only witness incapable of flattery.

**Recognition motifs:** Cord; pegs; marked board; measured movement; attention directed downward to terrain.

**On-page use:** Do not invent a grand architect aesthetic. Her authority is spatial literacy made visible.

## Roan

**Established visual signature:** Arrives after three months on the road with pack sheets using Stormrider measures; road-worn clothing in the integrated v1.7 appearance pass.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Roan looked travelled before he looked official. Road dust had worked into the seams of his coat, and the straps of his pack had polished pale tracks across the cloth. He carried paper that had crossed more communities than most people did and handled it like something meant to survive rain, hands, and disagreement. He said *we* before he said his name, and even his clothes seemed to belong to movement rather than a single room.

**Recognition motifs:** Road dust in seams; pack-strap wear; pack sheets; practical travelling layers; orientation toward groups.

**On-page use:** Avoid making him a nomadic stereotype. The visual point is accumulated travel and reciprocal work.

## Nella of Duskport

**Established visual signature:** No physical on-page appearance in Book One. She is present through paper, factors, routes, credit, and remembered instruction.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Deferred. Book One does not physically place Nella in the reader's sight. Her absence is useful: readers first know the shape of her mind through contracts, factors, return loads, and the refusal to let charity erase accounts.

**Recognition motifs:** For Book One, documentary rather than bodily: waxed folds, open-value contracts, name lines, return-load logic.

**On-page use:** Do not invent physical features retroactively. Establish them only when Nella appears directly in governing prose.

## Jeren Tesk

**Established visual signature:** Twenty-two; valley-born; second season on Whitehart road watch; crossbowman; wet ground affects his footing.

**Painterly prose target:**  
At twenty-two, Jeren could look younger from below than the neat facts in Wren's count made him sound. His road-watch coat sat wet at the hem, and he held the crossbow with the over-care of a man who knew the weapon mattered and had not yet learned how little control that gave him once a crowd began moving. A single slipped foot on wet turf registered because Wren was watching closely enough to see it.

**Recognition motifs:** Youth; road-watch coat; crossbow held close; unstable wet footing; tension visible through care.

**On-page use:** Do not make him visually villainous. The tragedy requires an ordinary watchman making a consequential decision.

## Joric Vale

**Established visual signature:** Mounted captain; physically impatient in the saddle; reins, horse, and downhill orientation dominate his first major scene.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Joric Vale seemed to sit forward even before there was somewhere to go. In the saddle his body was an argument for movement: reins shortened, weight pitched toward the downhill path, attention already beyond the line Aren wanted held. The urgency made him legible from a distance. When Aren stopped him, the stillness looked imposed rather than chosen.

**Recognition motifs:** Forward saddle posture; shortened reins; downhill gaze; arrested motion.

**On-page use:** Do not build him as a swaggering caricature. His physical impatience should arise from a defensible military reading of danger.

## Corrin

**Established visual signature:** Bad leg; split staff; beard; voice built to carry; later facial/mouth injury and permanently altered voice after Tam's assault.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Corrin entered a gathering through sound before shape. His bad leg put part of his weight onto a split staff, but the voice travelled without needing the rest of him to follow. A beard framed the mouth that made borrowed words feel older than they were. After Tam's assault the face and voice could no longer be separated from consequence: blood in the beard, broken support, and a performance instrument permanently changed by another person's rage.

**Recognition motifs:** Staff; uneven weight; beard; carrying voice; later damaged mouth/voice.

**On-page use:** Never let the later injury become a cosmetic badge. It changes livelihood, identity, and household economy.

## Roderick

**Established visual signature:** Right shoulder bound from an earlier bridge rescue; old damage tightens when seated; rescue work tears his coat; rope and boots dominate action.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Roderick's injury announced itself before he did. The wrapping around his right shoulder pulled his coat slightly crooked and held that arm nearer the body than the other, while everything else about him moved as though the damage belonged to someone he had no time to consult. Rope crossed him, mud found his boots, and rescue work eventually left a piece of his coat in a frightened boy's hand. He looked less like invulnerability than a man accustomed to deciding what could still be used.

**Recognition motifs:** Bound right shoulder; crooked coat; rope; planted boots; old rescue damage.

**On-page use:** Keep injury consequential. Do not turn pain tolerance into superhuman toughness.

## Elya Serenel

**Established visual signature:** Strong dry grip; Serenel eyes recognizable through family resemblance; eye corners fold in the same place as Alaine's when amused.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Elya's resemblance to Alaine did not arrive as a full copied face. It appeared in fragments: the corners of the eyes folding in the same place when amusement escaped, the Serenel gaze becoming unmistakable once Sela knew to look, a strong dry hand offered without ceremony. At first Sela could almost lose the resemblance by searching for it. Then Elya laughed, and family history crossed the face faster than explanation.

**Recognition motifs:** Serenel eyes; eye-corner folds; strong dry grip; resemblance that appears in expression rather than static features.

**On-page use:** Preserve partial resemblance. Do not make Serenels visually identical or biologically symbolic.

## Nessa

**Established visual signature:** Arrives carrying flour sack against hip; rain shines on cloak shoulders; touch is controlled and socially constrained.

**Painterly prose target:**  
Nessa's body knew the flour route before the conversation began. She carried the sack low against her hip, keeping the weight close, rain bright on the shoulders of her cloak. In public she made herself careful: a thumb against Fen's finger could plausibly be balance, a thank-you could be aimed at the space beside him, a face could refuse the smile that private history might have permitted. Work made her visible; restraint made the history between them visible only to those who already knew it.

**Recognition motifs:** Flour sack at hip; rain-dark cloak shoulders; controlled touch; withheld smile; practical carrying posture.

**On-page use:** Avoid romantic beauty cues. The scene should preserve the cost of public social distance.

## Col

**Established visual signature:** Seventeen at the trial; brown coat; newly blue-patched elbow; rubs the patch with two fingers; watches the lamp.

**Painterly prose target:**  
At seventeen Col had not yet grown beyond the coat his household still repaired for him. The brown wool carried a new blue patch at one elbow, bright enough in lamplight that Tomas remembered it sixteen years after forgetting the exact question. Col rubbed the patch with two fingers through most of the first hour and watched the lamp more than the Tester. The remembered body survives in fragments because that is all the record allows: blue cloth moving at the edge of light, quick answers, a boy trying not to look uncertain.

**Recognition motifs:** Brown coat; blue elbow patch; two-finger rubbing gesture; lamp-facing attention; remembered youth.

**On-page use:** Preserve the fragmentary nature of the memory. Never make visual memory prove survival, guilt, metaphysics, or Tomas's correctness.
