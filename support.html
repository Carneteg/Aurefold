Annotated page · craft note

Why the book opens on an argument, not the goatherd

Book One doesn't begin with Sela. It begins in a sealed room, far from the valley, where three officials argue over a record they cannot fully trust. The choice is deliberate. Before you meet the girl, you meet the machine that will decide what she means — the witnessing, the wax seals, the burned trial notes, the sentence that closes every question by calling it answered.

What that does to a reader, and why the whole novel is built to make you feel the record before you feel the person, is the part I want to walk through — including the two lines I cut, and the one I almost cut and didn't…

Worldbuilding · house deep-dive

What House Whitehart is really afraid of

Whitehart is called the house of faith, but faith is not what it guards. It exists to test — to stand between a person who says they heard something and a world that badly wants the question settled. A house built to doubt on everyone's behalf is a strange kind of church.

This note goes into how the listening house actually works — the testing rite, why the valley both needs it and resents it, and the quiet fear that runs under all of it: what happens the day the testers are wrong…

What support does — and does not — buy

Members receive closer access to the making of Aurefold. They do not purchase authority over its canon. The ten Houses, the characters, and the unresolved mysteries remain the responsibility of the author. Community polls may shape which supplemental topic, visual concept, or production note is shared next, but never what becomes true inside the story.

Questions

Is the book written?

Yes. The Bell of Silence is a completed manuscript of approximately 86,000 words.

What stage is it in?

Internal developmental revision is complete. Next comes external reader testing, then professional proofreading, cover and interior design, and print preparation.

Why use Patreon before crowdfunding?

Patreon lets early readers follow the work and helps build the community that makes a later campaign viable. It supports the preparation itself.

What will crowdfunding pay for?

The final production: proofreading, cover and interior design, typesetting, print proofs, and fulfilment. Exact costs will come from real quotes, not guesses.

Does membership influence the story?

No. Members get closer access to how Aurefold is made — never authority over its canon.

Will members receive the complete manuscript?

No. Selected excerpts are shared. The full manuscript is not released.

Are physical rewards available now?

No. Physical rewards belong to the future crowdfunding campaign, once printing, packaging, and shipping have been modelled.

When will crowdfunding launch?

There is no date yet. Free Patreon members are notified first.

Can I follow without paying?

Yes. Free membership (Traveller) gives you updates, selected excerpts, and campaign news.

Where can I read a sample?

On the Read page: the prologue and the opening of Chapter One.

A note from the author

Aurefold and The Bell of Silence are written by me, Tobias Carneteg.

I could try to publish this book quietly and show you only the finished object. I'd rather do it in the open — share the road from a completed manuscript to a printed book, the good weeks and the slow ones, and let readers be part of it before the cover is even designed.

The Bell of Silence is a story about testimony: about a person who will say only what she knows, and a world that would rather settle the meaning of a silence than live with an open question. It's the kind of book I most want to read, so it's the kind of book I set out to write.

If that sounds like something you'd want on your shelf, the best thing you can do right now costs nothing — follow along. Everything else grows from there.

— Tobias
