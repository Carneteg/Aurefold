Annotated page · craft note

Why the book opens on an argument, not the goatherd

Book One doesn't begin with Sela. It begins in a sealed room, far from the valley, where three officials argue over a record they cannot fully trust. The choice is deliberate. Before you meet the girl, you meet the machine that will decide what she means — the witnessing, the wax seals, the burned trial notes, the sentence that closes every question by calling it answered.

What that does to a reader, and why the whole novel is built to make you feel the record before you feel the person, is the part I want to walk through — including the two lines I cut, and the one I almost cut and didn't…

Worldbuilding · house deep-dive

What House Whitehart is really afraid of

Whitehart is called the house of faith, but faith is not what it guards. It exists to test — to stand between a person who says they heard something and a world that badly wants the question settled. A house built to doubt on everyone's behalf is a strange kind of church.

This note goes into how the listening house actually works — the testing rite, why the valley both needs it and resents it, and the quiet fear that runs under all of it: what happens the day the testers are wrong…

Where the money will go

When the crowdfunding campaign opens, every contribution goes into making and delivering the physical book — never into the story, which is already written. The work it pays for, roughly in order of cost:

  • Printing & production — the physical books themselves
  • Shipping & fulfilment — packing and posting worldwide
  • Editing & proofreading — professional passes before print
  • Cover & interior design — typesetting and layout
  • Platform & payment fees — the crowdfunding platform's share
  • Contingency — reprints, damaged copies, the unexpected

Exact figures will be published from real supplier quotes before the campaign opens — a full budget breakdown, not guesses.

What the campaign will offer

The crowdfunding campaign — separate from Patreon, and still in preparation — is where the book itself becomes available. Planned reward types include:

  • Digital edition — the eBook
  • Paperback — the printed book
  • Hardcover — a collector's edition
  • Signed copies — signed by the author
  • Named in the book — founding-patron recognition
  • Art & extras — map prints, bookplates, and more

These are planned, not final — nothing is on sale yet. Formats and prices are confirmed when the campaign launches, and free Patreon members hear first.

Lend your craft

Aurefold is being built in the open, and there's room for a few collaborators who want to help carry Book One to publication. These are pro bono roles — unpaid, but credited, with a real hand in how the book and its world reach readers. A few evenings a month is plenty.

  • Video editor — trailers, TikToks, and campaign video cut from existing footage and art
  • Lore master — keep continuity, grow the world archive, and field reader lore questions
  • Beta / manuscript readers — read the finished manuscript and give structured feedback before proofreading
  • Illustrator — cover, map, and spot art: the visual hooks the campaign is built on
  • Social & community manager — grow and run the TikTok, Discord, and Instagram, and steward the Moot, where readers help shape the world around the book
  • Audio manager — audiobook, trailer sound and music, and the audio side of social
  • Graphic designer — interior typesetting and crowdfunding key art

Interested? Write to tobias.carneteg@gmail.com with the role and a little about your work. Every collaborator is credited.

What support does — and does not — buy

Members receive closer access to the making of Aurefold. They do not purchase authority over its canon. The ten Houses, the central plot, the characters' fates, and the unresolved mysteries remain the responsibility of the author. What the Moot can shape is the world around the book — which supplemental material, art, or topic the archive opens next, and bounded extras like which side-story gets written or the name of a minor figure in the supplementary lore. The heart of the story stays the author's, and no vote is bought — one voice per reader.

Is the book written?

Yes. The Bell of Silence is a completed manuscript of approximately 86,000 words.

What stage is it in?

Internal developmental revision is complete. Next comes external reader testing, then professional proofreading, cover and interior design, and print preparation.

Why use Patreon before crowdfunding?

Patreon lets early readers follow the work and helps build the community that makes a later campaign viable. It supports the preparation itself.

What will crowdfunding pay for?

The final production: proofreading, cover and interior design, typesetting, print proofs, and fulfilment. Exact costs will come from real quotes, not guesses.

Does membership influence the story?

The central plot, canon, and the book's unresolved mysteries are the author's alone. What the community can shape through the Moot is the world around the book — which material or art the archive opens next, and bounded extras like which side-story gets written or a minor figure's name in the supplementary lore. Membership brings closer access to how Aurefold is made, not authority over its canon.

No. Selected excerpts are shared. The full manuscript is not released.

A note from the author

Aurefold and The Bell of Silence are written by me, Tobias Carneteg.

I could try to publish this book quietly and show you only the finished object. I'd rather do it in the open — share the road from a completed manuscript to a printed book, the good weeks and the slow ones, and let readers be part of it before the cover is even designed.

The Bell of Silence is a story about testimony: about a person who will say only what she knows, and a world that would rather settle the meaning of a silence than live with an open question. It's the kind of book I most want to read, so it's the kind of book I set out to write.

If that sounds like something you'd want on your shelf, the best thing you can do right now costs nothing — follow along. Everything else grows from there.

— Tobias
