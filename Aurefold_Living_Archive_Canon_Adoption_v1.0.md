# AUREFOLD — Living Archive Cycle: Canon Adoption Record v1.0

**Purpose.** The source file *AUREFOLD Master Lore File — Living Archive Cycle*
was supplied with status **"PROPOSAL COMPILATION / Canonical authority: None."**
Its own header states that its names, dates, institutions, events, and quotations
"remain PROPOSAL unless formally adopted into the Aurefold Constitution and Canon
Ledger." This record performs that adoption for the purpose of publishing a
spoiler-safe public presentation, and logs the continuity checks behind it.

**Authority / precedence used.** Constitution v1.8 → Canon Ledger v1.8 → Series
Architecture → Book appendices → Master manuscript. This record sits at Ledger
level as an addendum; it does **not** override anything above it.

**Scope of adoption.** Ten House-centred historical "Living Archive" entries
(188–394 A.U.) plus one realm-wide entry (229 A.U.), adopted as **contested
historical backstory** — i.e. in-world documents that preserve conflict and are
explicitly *not* single official truth. Nothing here touches Book One's plot or
its protected endings.

**`[CANON DELTA]`** — the following are logged as newly adopted canon backstory.

## Global continuity findings

- **Consistent with the locked house philosophies** (Spoiler Matrix v1.0 §3,
  "Ten Houses + philosophies, LOCKED CANON"). Each entry dramatises the house's
  established virtue *and* its cost, which is the series' load-bearing structure
  ("which house would you follow," not "which house is good"). No philosophy is
  reassigned.
- **Respects the protected set** (Spoiler Matrix §1). All entries are historical
  (pre-396 A.U.) and touch none of the Book One protected items (Sela's blank
  page, the Bell's final strokes, the gate casualty accounting, Col's fate, any
  named ending).
- **Eleventh House / Ashen Crown left unresolved** (Matrix §6). Entry XI keeps
  "the eleventh seat… unoccupied and unexplained." No magic, prophecy,
  resurrection, or afterlife is confirmed; death remains permanent. Compliant.
- **Tenfold Compact framing preserved** — decentralised, no emperor/throne/army;
  the "Hall of Eleven" language (eleven places, ten recognised voices) is used
  consistently in Entry XI.
- **`[CONTINUITY RISK — low]`** These entries were reconciled against the Spoiler
  Matrix and the public site's locked facts, **not** line-by-line against the
  full Constitution v1.8 and Canon Ledger v1.8 `.docx`. Before final ratification
  into the master Ledger, run a proper-noun and date pass against those two files
  (new names below; new places: the Narrow Fields, Red Hollow, Three Spears,
  Saint Orra's House, Brightward, the Quiet Office, South Hall, the Guild of
  Measures, the Admiralty Chamber of Charts, the Civic Futures Assembly).
- **Cross-link:** Entry IX ("every chart after it must name its date, maker,
  purpose, access class, and deliberate omissions") satisfies the outstanding
  map-provenance requirement in Spoiler Matrix §4.

## Public-presentation decision

Per Spoiler Matrix §5 the *full* fragments (letters, hearing transcripts) are
**member-tier depth**, and several contain mature content (a coerced marriage, a
sealed assault testimony, wartime deaths). The **public** site therefore carries
only a **distilled, tasteful** form of each entry — title, house, year, and a
two-to-three-sentence account that preserves the ambiguity without the graphic
detail — presented on `history.html` as contested archive fragments. The full
text remains available for the member/reviewer tiers if desired.
