The Banner

The Banner is the Aurefold reader community — the place where readers stop being only readers. Find the House whose instincts most resemble your own, swear in if you choose, and take your place in the company around the book.

Content note. Aurefold is a mature series intended for adult readers — discussion and reader material may touch graphic violence and other adult themes.

Everything under the Banner lives around the story — side-stories, extras, public lore, questions, theories and reader work. The central plot, canon, protected mysteries and character fates stay the author's. New voices are read by the archivist before they show.

Find Your House Twelve difficult choices. Ten defensible philosophies. Discover which Great House your instincts lean toward. No House is the right answer. Every answer protects something — and costs something. The Moot Vote on what the archive opens next — and answer the Ledger Question that divides the company. One reader, one voice. The archive counts; it does not decide the book's canon. The Banner Hall Raise a character of your own, sworn to a house — the finest are honoured, the very finest taken into the material. In the hall already: Yara Cobbleworth of Stormrider, Corin Farrow of Ashbourne, and Old Ferro of Ironvale — sworn and honoured. The Scriptorium Leave a story, a theory, a piece of art, or a question for the author. Lately: the theory “Why the Chronicle and the Ash Fragments Can't Both Be Lying,” and the story “A Night at the Toll House.”
