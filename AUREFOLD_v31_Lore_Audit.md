## Approved charged elevens in the manuscript

The manuscript contains no accidental new eleven-pattern. The charged uses are limited to:

- the authorised prologue's eleven lords,
- the damaged-seal claim that “some say eleven,”
- Bryn Corr's deliberate eleven-to-nine miscount.

The heading `Chapter Eleven` is structural, not diegetic.
